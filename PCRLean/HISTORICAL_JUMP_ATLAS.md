# PCR-JUMP-LEAN Historical Jump Atlas

## Status

This file is an isolated research-generation document.  Historical analogies generate candidate definitions; they are not theorems and do not enter the certified dependency graph.

## 1. Coordinate and representation jumps

### Analytic geometry

**Old obstruction.** Synthetic incidence arguments did not scale uniformly to complicated curves.

**Jump.** Replace geometric configurations by polynomial equations and geometric operations by algebraic elimination.

**Reusable mechanism.** Move the problem to a representation in which the desired operation becomes functorial and finite.

**PCR translation.** Replace an unstable scalar such as residual order by a finite Frobenius--Hasse/Rees module whose transformation can be expressed by homomorphisms.

**Required bridge.** The module must determine an actual finite-type centre and have an all-chart transform law.

### Fourier and spectral transforms

**Old obstruction.** Local differential interactions were not monotone in physical coordinates.

**Jump.** Diagonalize the evolution in a transform domain.

**PCR translation.** Generate the full differential/Cartier operator orbit of a packet and study its stable annihilator rather than one chosen derivative or direction.

**Required bridge.** Operator-stable annihilators must descend to regular subbundles and actual centre ideals.

## 2. Symmetry and structural jumps

### Galois theory

**Old obstruction.** Direct formulas for roots failed in higher degree.

**Jump.** Replace formulas by the symmetry group controlling all roots simultaneously.

**PCR translation.** Replace arbitrary singleton centre selection by a symmetry-stable finite centre word or invariant generator frame.

**Required bridge.** Equivariant local frames must glue to one actual ideal or a finite invariant serialization.

### Noetherian algebra

**Old obstruction.** Infinite generation made direct termination arguments impossible.

**Jump.** Prove that every ascending chain stabilizes instead of bounding every coefficient explicitly.

**PCR translation.** Store all genuinely new causal traces in one fixed Noetherian ancestor module.  Independent births strictly enlarge the generated trace submodule, hence cannot occur infinitely often.

**Required bridge.** Every geometric birth must map functorially to that fixed module, and dependent births must pay a separate local rank.

## 3. New-object and category jumps

### Schemes

**Old obstruction.** Varieties forgot nilpotents, specializations and arithmetic fibres needed by intersection and deformation arguments.

**Jump.** Enlarge the category so that the missing infinitesimal data become actual points and sheaves.

**PCR translation.** Retain nonreduced direction schemes, Frobenius thickenings, Fitting schemes and odd-contact towers instead of reducing them prematurely.

**Required bridge.** The enlarged diagnostic object must be algebraized back to an ordinary coherent ideal and regular centre.

### Logarithmic and toroidal geometry

**Old obstruction.** Boundary degenerations were treated as external bookkeeping.

**Jump.** Put the boundary into the geometric object itself.

**PCR translation.** Centres, owners, exceptional ancestry and contact debt must be objects in a log/toroidal state, not scalar annotations.

**Required bridge.** Toroidal principalization centres must pass all active/passive gates and compile back to ordinary blowups.

## 4. Local-to-global and obstruction jumps

### Sheaves and cohomology

**Old obstruction.** Local solutions existed but failed to glue for invisible global reasons.

**Jump.** Represent failure of gluing by a cohomology class.

**PCR translation.** Local Frobenius roots or conormal graph equations define a Cech torsor; vanishing produces an actual root ideal, while a nonzero class is a typed obstruction rather than a guessed centre.

**Required bridge.** Blowup must either kill the class, lower its support, or transfer it to a strictly lower packet.

### Descent theory

**Old obstruction.** Objects constructed after a cover had no canonical global form.

**Jump.** Isolate overlap cocycles and effective descent conditions.

**PCR translation.** Finite generator frames related by invertible changes must define one chart-independent ideal.

**Required bridge.** The transition matrices must be produced canonically after every blowup chart and remain compatible under cleaning and saturation.

## 5. Dynamical and monotonicity jumps

### Morse theory and geometric flows

**Old obstruction.** Static classification gave no mechanism for reaching canonical form.

**Jump.** Introduce a flow and an energy or index that controls critical transitions.

**PCR translation.** Regard blowup as a discrete flow.  The Lyapunov object may be a Noetherian submodule plus a local rank, a packet multiset, or an SCC escape tree rather than one scalar.

**Required bridge.** Every legal geometric step must be classified as an outer Noetherian enlargement or an inner strict local drop.

### Perelman-style entropy plus surgery

**Old obstruction.** Singularities may form even under a monotone flow.

**Jump.** Combine a global monotone quantity with a finite list of controlled singular models and surgery.

**PCR translation.** Combine a global Cartier/causal memory with certified local centre words for cusp, quadratic, radicial and Fitting-kernel chambers.

**Required bridge.** A finite packetization theorem must show that every singularity enters one certified surgery chamber.

## 6. Deformation, lifting and patching jumps

### Hensel and deformation lifting

**Old obstruction.** A solution existed only in the tangent or residue object.

**Jump.** Identify and kill obstruction groups to lift the solution.

**PCR translation.** Extract a centre from the associated graded or conormal packet, then lift it through finite thickenings using an explicit obstruction complex.

**Required bridge.** Vanishing must produce a coherent ideal in the original finite-type scheme, not only in the completion.

### Taylor--Wiles patching

**Old obstruction.** Local deformation conditions did not by themselves imply the global theorem.

**Jump.** Add auxiliary levels, build finite patched modules, and use a numerical/structural criterion.

**PCR translation.** Prove all local chamber modules, then patch them through one Noetherian ancestor-memory and one global centre atlas.

**Required bridge.** Local packet transitions must be compatible on overlaps and every unmatched birth must enlarge the fixed patched module.

## 7. Invariant jumps

### Homology, K-theory and index theory

**Old obstruction.** Direct geometry changed under deformation while a hidden obstruction remained stable.

**Jump.** Pass to a functorial algebraic invariant with exact sequences.

**PCR translation.** Use the cotangent/Fitting complex, Cartier crystal, or causal trace module so that each blowup produces an exact triangle or finite filtration.

**Required bridge.** Strict geometric improvement must correspond to deletion of a nonzero subquotient, and finite length must prevent infinite repetition.

## 8. Computation and formalization jumps

### Four-colour, Kepler and proof assistants

**Old obstruction.** The proof depended on a large finite configuration space too error-prone for unaided checking.

**Jump.** Separate conceptual reduction from exhaustive certified verification.

**PCR translation.** Use mathematical theorems to reduce arbitrary inputs to finite packet grammars; use Lean to verify every chart, overlap, rank edge and compiler theorem.

**Required bridge.** Finite verification cannot replace the universal reduction theorem.

## 9. Direct precedents in resolution

### Hironaka

**Jump components.** Marked objects, maximal contact, coefficient ideals, exceptional history and dimension induction.

**Positive-characteristic lesson.** The architecture remains powerful, but maximal contact and scalar monotonicity fail.  A replacement must be intrinsic under Frobenius and history-sensitive.

### Characteristic polyhedra and idealistic filtrations

**Jump components.** Encode many coefficients and differential consequences in one structured object.

**Remaining issue.** Termination and kangaroo phenomena; a packet may recur after cleaning and exceptional changes.

### Hauser--Perlega cycles

**Lesson.** A chosen small centre and residual order may cycle, while a larger permissible centre may escape.  The correct obstruction is centre grammar plus history, not merely local order.
