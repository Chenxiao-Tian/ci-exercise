# X052 Single-Target Focus Freeze

## Defectless Projection Existence (DPE)

All further work on the X052 line is frozen to the following theorem until it is proved, refuted, or decomposed by a genuine counterexample.

> Let `k` be perfect and `(F|k,v)` a valued function field. There exists a separating transcendence basis `t=(t_1,...,t_d)` such that the chosen henselian branch of `F^h|k(t)^h` is defectless.

### Minimal proof cut

```text
DPE-1  parameterize admissible finite separable projections and their local henselian branches;
DPE-2  prove the minimum projection defect is zero outside the known Abhyankar/tame chambers;
DPE-3  analyze the rank-one transcendence-defect-one chamber without extending F;
DPE-4  obtain uniformity on quasi-compact valuation neighbourhoods;
DPE-5  preserve source/projection ancestry under common refinements;
DPE-6  exclude projection-minimal positive-defect cores or construct an explicit counterexample.
```

### Forbidden diversions

Do not introduce:

- a model-dependent defect counter;
- a coarsening or approximation counter;
- a count of attempted projections;
- a new source ledger or centre grammar;
- another manuscript architecture;
- an alteration-field projection in place of a projection of the original function field.

### Immediate work order

1. Start with rank-one transcendence-defect-one valuations.
2. Extract a standard valuation transcendence basis for the Abhyankar part.
3. Compare the remaining immediate trdeg-one extension with Kuhlmann henselian rationality.
4. Identify precisely whether the required tame-base extension can be descended to the original rational subfield.
5. Prove defectless projection or record the minimal obstruction as a theorem/counterexample.

General positive-characteristic resolution remains open.
