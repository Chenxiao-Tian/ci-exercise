# X062 Supplement

## Conservative Blowup Recollement and Exceptional Event Descent

### 1. Standard geometric input

For the blowup of a regular centre, the complement of the exceptional divisor is the unchanged open and the exceptional divisor is a smooth projective bundle over the centre. Open-closed recollement gives two canonical triangles with `i^*`, `i^!`, and the open restriction. These standard inputs are cited immediately in the public article.

### 2. Conservativity

If `j^*K=0` and `i^*K=0`, the first recollement triangle gives `K=0`. If `j^*K=0` and `i^!K=0`, the second gives the same conclusion. Applying this to a cone proves conservativity on morphisms.

### 3. Iteration

A finite blowup word produces a finite tree. Every closed child is supported on an exceptional boundary stratum and has smaller carrier dimension. Source labels identify copies across charts and common refinements.

### 4. Exact event descent

At a maximal node, the algebraic actual-centre word makes the successor map epimorphic in the generic Serre quotient. A nonzero open event yields a nonzero kernel and a length drop. If the open event vanishes, recollement forces a nonzero exceptional or gluing term, which is lower-dimensional.

### 5. Why raw direct image is insufficient

A nonzero exceptional coherent complex can have zero derived pushforward. X062 therefore retains root direct image only as one component of the conservative probe. The proof never states or uses conservativity of proper direct image.
