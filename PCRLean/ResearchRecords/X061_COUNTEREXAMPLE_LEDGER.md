# X061 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. Finite stratification is not finite monodromy

A fixed stratum in characteristic p may carry infinitely many finite local systems. The proof does not choose a common trivializing cover.

## 2. Finite length of each stage is not automatically a common budget

The rank requires a strict quotient map from the current leading object to the successor. Merely recording that both objects have finite length is insufficient.

## 3. Equal abstract cohomology lists do not determine a derived object

The proof uses the actual comparison morphism. If all induced perverse cohomology maps are isomorphisms, the morphism is an isomorphism; otherwise a first failure is visible.

## 4. A total support order breaks symmetry

All maximal incomparable supports and their intersections are treated simultaneously.

## 5. Constructible support is not a scheme-theoretic centre

Actual centres come from Cartier-dual coherent quotients, Fitting ideals, marked Hasse jets, passive exactification, and boundary legality.

## 6. Smooth pullback can split supports

Pullback components are aliases with one groupoid descent identity. They do not receive independent source budgets.

## 7. Normalization is not a final modification

Kummer normalization and normalized Rees blowups are comparison devices. The output uses ordinary blowups in regular centres.

## 8. Validation boundary

The public theorem is unconditional under the user's X061 assumption. The new global dynamic-support bridges have not been independently certified in this archive.
