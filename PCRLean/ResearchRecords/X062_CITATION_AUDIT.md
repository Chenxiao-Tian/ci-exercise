## Attribution boundary

The cited sources are used only for their stated standard results. The following are presented as X062 theorems rather than attributed to those sources:

- minimal-carrier matching across the algebraic--constructible bridge;
- Fitting-exact generic annihilation for the complete resolution state;
- support-causal successor descent;
- compilation of these results with Cartier--Spencer--Kummer descent and the dynamic support cycle into arbitrary-dimensional ordinary-blowup resolution.

## Bibliographic style

The article uses `amsart` and `amsplain`. Journal articles include journal, volume, year, and pages when available; recent preprints include title, authors, year, arXiv identifier, and subject class. Stacks Project citations use precise tags at the point of use.
