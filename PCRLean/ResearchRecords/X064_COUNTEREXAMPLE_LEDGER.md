# X064 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

1. **Support dimension is not ambient dimension.** Every nonzero ideal on an integral smooth `n`-fold has proper support; this does not make its principalization a lower-dimensional theorem.
2. **A legal ambient lift is not yet a progress theorem.** The normal-coefficient bridge must identify controlled transforms of the complete packet.
3. **The radicial-envelope theorem contains local uniformization.** Its proof must be audited as the central theorem, not treated as a short technical lemma.
4. **Kangaroo residual-order increase.** Contact order may rise after blowup. The compensated state must pay for it by a strict cleaning quotient.
5. **Hauser--Perlega same-form cycle.** Formal shape recurrence is not eliminated by declaring gauge-equivalent returns irrelevant.
6. **Fixed projection firewall.** Blowups do not change the defect of a fixed valued-field extension.
7. **Proper pushforward is not conservative.** Exceptional events require open--exceptional recollement.
8. **Recollement trees are factorization dependent.** The event rank uses factorization-independent abstract blowup cubes.
9. **Short-proof density is only diagnostic.** It does not prove falsehood, but it identifies where line-by-line external review must concentrate.
10. **Archive truth boundary.** The assumed-complete public mode is not independent certification.
