# MLEL-X046 Supplement

## Prime-Layer Defect Classification and Differential-Ramification Shadows

**Parent:** MLEL-X046 / PDS-AST-IDE-FINAL  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** standard valuation-theoretic reduction plus finite packet interface; geometric effectivity open.

---

## 1. Prime-layer tower

For a finite valued-field extension `L/K`, separate the purely inseparable part and pass to a finite normal closure of the separable part. Choose a composition series of the finite Galois group and refine the inseparable degree into prime-degree radicial steps. This gives a finite tower whose nontrivial layers have prime degree.

Ramification indices, residue degrees and defect factors are multiplicative in finite towers. Therefore nontrivial total defect appears in a prime layer. The tower is not unique, so the X046 token retains its common refinement class and the multiset of typed prime layers rather than a preferred composition series.

---

## 2. Layer types

### 2.1 Defectless layer

A defectless projection can enter the multiplicity-reduction algorithm of Cutkosky--Mourtada. Abhyankar/quasi-monomial valuations enter the Knaf--Kuhlmann monomialization route. These are positive geometric exits.

### 2.2 Purely inseparable layer

A prime purely inseparable layer is enrolled as a Frobenius/radicial packet. Its finite shadows are Hasse coefficient ideals, Frobenius exponents, radicial conductors and the source-labelled normalization debt. The existing radicial branch is responsible for an ordinary-centre realization on the original model.

### 2.3 Dependent Artin--Schreier defect

The dependent class is linked, in the valuation-theoretic classification, to a purely inseparable immediate extension. X046 therefore pairs the Artin--Schreier equation with the related radicial shadow and treats the layer as a two-colour radicial packet. This is a classification reduction, not by itself an actual-centre theorem.

### 2.4 Independent prime defect

Independent defect is retained by a finite portfolio rather than one scalar:

```text
ramification ideal shadows;
annihilator and Fitting data of relative Kahler differentials;
trace images of maximal and ramification ideals;
coefficient-distance and key-degree shadows;
Dedekind/different shadows;
source, projection and common-model ancestry.
```

Known prime-degree characterizations of independent defect use these objects. No one component is declared complete outside its exact theorem.

---

## 3. Higher-degree firewall

Nontrivial defect in a degree-`p^2` extension need not force a nonprincipal ramification ideal. Consequently:

```text
nonprincipal ramification ideal
  => useful independent-defect signal in declared prime chambers,

trivial or principal ramification ideal
  !=> defectless in arbitrary higher degree.
```

The prime-layer tower and the full differential-ramification portfolio are mandatory.

---

## 4. Source and factorization invariance

Changing a prime tower by refinement or by a common Galois closure does not create fresh sources. A refined layer inherits the source of the extension packet it refines. The event-octahedron composes layer shadows; equivalent towers are compared on a common refinement. The global rank uses the multiset of typed layer profiles, not the number of a chosen factorization's steps.

---

## 5. Remaining geometric interface

Valuation classification does not construct a regular jointly legal centre. The geometric theorem must turn a nonzero differential-ramification shadow on a finite model into an X044 coloured centre block and prove that the successor shadow is lower on every valuation of the certified neighbourhood. This is IDE.
