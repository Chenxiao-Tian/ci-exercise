# X053 Counterexample and Failure-Boundary Ledger

## 1. Quasi-compactness does not regularize a common model

The blowup of `A^2` in `(x^2,y^3)` has a chart

```text
k[x,y,u]/(y^3-x^2u),
```

singular at the origin. Therefore a finite Riemann--Zariski subcover followed by an arbitrary common blowup is not a resolution proof.

## 2. A proper model dominating regular local models can be singular

A proper birational modification of a regular scheme may be singular when its centre ideal is not regular. Domination of each local uniformization model does not imply regularity of the common model.

## 3. Concatenating local words can destroy regularity

After applying one source word, the strict transform of a centre from another word need not be regular or transverse to the resolved strict transform. A common-refinement proof must retain the complete terminal portfolio and regularize intersections before blowing them up.

## 4. Pointwise projection choice is not functorial

A finite subcover extracted from a quasi-compact space is not canonical. A smooth pullback can admit a different finite subcover. Pointwise DPE plus compactness gives existence but not smooth-functoriality.

## 5. Constructibility of the projection choice is unnecessary for finite existence

A fixed finite word has an open terminal basin whether or not the set of projections producing it is constructible. Requiring a constructible projection family at the valuation-theoretic input level is stronger than needed for existence.

## 6. A source modification ideal does not replace source-word semantics

Different centre words can have isomorphic composite modifications but different strict transforms, especially in Cartier identity examples. The common ideal is used only to dominate the modifications; ordered centres and terminal payloads remain part of the portfolio.

## 7. Product invertibility requires componentwise nonzerodivisor control

On a ring with zero divisors, invertibility of a product does not automatically imply invertibility of every factor. The factorization argument is made componentwise in total quotient rings and treats ideals that are units on a component separately.

## 8. Valuation coverage detects points only after hereditary preservation

A valuation whose source centre lies in a regular terminal open does not by itself make its centre on an arbitrary dominating model regular. The intervening centres must be disjoint from or cleanly transverse to the terminal pair. This is the role of the hereditary common-refinement theorem.

## 9. Local uniformization and global resolution are not formally identical without patching

Published local-uniformization arguments explicitly note that arbitrary local uniformizations do not automatically patch in higher dimension. X053's implication is conditional on the specific regular common-refinement architecture; it is not attributed to compactness alone.

## 10. Truth boundary

Pointwise DPE remains open. The regular common-refinement theorem remains conditional on X040--X044. Smooth-functorial projection-atlas selection remains open. General positive-characteristic resolution is not established.
