## Proof-length diagnostic

A mechanical count gives 418 proof environments, median approximately 62 prose words, with 279 proofs at or below 80 words and 3 proofs above 300 words. This statistic is not a disproof. It does show that a reviewer should focus first on the long load-bearing proofs and then check every short theorem which those proofs cite.
