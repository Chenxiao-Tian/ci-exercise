# X062 Last-Chance Proof Audit

## Audit question

Does the constructible leading event used by the dynamic termination rank coincide with the coherent algebraic defect actually eliminated by the ordinary centre word?

## Result

X062 answers this through the unique minimal Cartier representative and its intrinsic Fitting tower.

### Closed standard inputs

- Minimal Cartier modules and equivalence with Cartier crystals: Blickle--Boeckle, Section 3, especially Lemmas 3.8--3.9 and Theorems 3.10--3.12.
- Fitting ideals are finite type and commute with base change: Stacks Project, Tags 0C3E and 07ZD.
- Flattening by blowup: Raynaud--Gruson; Stacks Project, Tag 0815.
- Fitting blowup and locally free strict transform: Stacks Project, Tag 0ESN.
- Strict transform and etale localization: Stacks Project, Tags 080D and 0863.
- Scheme-theoretic image of strict transform: Stacks Project, Tag 0F3Y.

### X062 integrated bridges

1. The leading perverse kernel is the solution of a residual Cartier quotient with the contravariant direction correctly reversed.
2. Minimalization makes the Fitting carrier independent of nilpotent presentation noise.
3. Complete Fitting-Rees preparation makes the minimal quotient flat and generically zero over the support.
4. No successor component dominates the old support.
5. Every new event lies on a proper support descendant.
6. The dynamic Serre-quotient length therefore strictly decreases.

## Firewalls checked

- no Fitting ideal is formed from an arbitrary Cartier representative;
- no crystal-theoretic zero is treated as actual zero before minimalization;
- no singular flattening centre is counted as an allowed centre;
- no set-theoretic closure replaces strict transform;
- no constructible support is used directly as a centre;
- no finite-monodromy or finite-Ext envelope is assumed;
- no valuation chooses a centre.
