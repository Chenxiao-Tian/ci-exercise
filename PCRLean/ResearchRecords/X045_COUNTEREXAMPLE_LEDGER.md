# X045 Counterexample and Failure-Boundary Ledger

## 1. No-recharge is not no-infinite-birth

An arbitrarily long age-increasing chain can consume every old token at most once while creating one fresh jump-capable token at each stage. Therefore an acyclic birth DAG and no double payment do not imply termination. X045 must prohibit new root source IDs, not merely recharge of old IDs.

## 2. A new exceptional divisor is not a new source

The Whitney point-centre loop produces new exceptional divisors indefinitely. Assigning one free source coupon to every divisor creates unlimited capacity. In X045 an exceptional component inherits the join of the colours that created its centre; it is never a fresh root.

## 3. Chart copies are not births

One global event sheaf may restrict to several nonzero chart pieces or decompose into several irreducible components. Counting each piece as a source clones one event. The primitive source-filtered event packet, not a chart component, is the canonical identity.

## 4. Split-merge cycles

On a finite source universe `{a,b}`, a policy allowing

```text
{a,b} -> {a}+{b} -> {a,b}
```

can cycle forever. Primitive join quotients prevent the join source from being redefined as two clones. Lower-support primitive quotients are separate older source packets, not children that replenish the join.

## 5. Same Fitting ideals do not determine the event complex

Two coherent complexes can have the same support and Fitting ideals but different extension classes. Hence X045 no-upward birth cannot rely only on support/Fitting profiles. It requires the complete-colour presentation complex and the event comparison map. If all complete presentation data and every prepared comparison agree, the cone is zero; a nonzero cone must be assigned to a typed colour or emitted as `UNCLASSIFIED-COHERENT-EVENT`.

## 6. Omitted coherent colours destroy no-upward birth

If successor recomputation is allowed to emit a new coherent obstruction not enrolled in the X044 portfolio, it can have equal or higher rank. Semantic completeness of the finite coherent grammar is therefore load bearing and remains a stated hypothesis of the X045 coherent termination theorem.

## 7. Local mode decrease does not compose automatically

Alternating maps such as

```text
(a,b) -> (a-1,b+2),
(a,b) -> (a+2,b-1)
```

show that each step lowering some coordinate is not a global rank. X045 uses a fixed token order and the Dershowitz--Manna replacement condition on every complete block.

## 8. Immediate defect is not a coherent event support

An independent immediate-defect ramification cut can be nonprincipal in its valuation ring. The extension of a finitely generated ideal from a Noetherian model is principal in the valuation ring, so no exact coherent ideal represents the cut. Class V must not be forced into the finite coherent event grammar.

## 9. Cofinal-tail shift is not progress

A pro-object and every cofinal tail are isomorphic. The number of approximants consumed, one key-polynomial index, or a tail shift cannot be an intrinsic strict rank.

## 10. Fixed-projection defect is not a model-stage rank

For a fixed finite valued-field extension and fixed valuation extension, field degree, ramification index, residue degree, and defect do not change under birational modification of the models. A valid class-V escape must change an independent coordinate, the projection, or the pro-isomorphism class.

## 11. Finite constructible observables are eventually constant

Along an immediate approximation tail converging in the constructible topology, every invariant factoring through a fixed finite constructible partition is eventually constant. UDE-EV must act uniformly on a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood, not select one finite approximation.

## 12. Public truth boundary

The event octahedron and finite source filtration are standard or finite constructions. Coherent no-upward birth is conditional on complete-colour semantics and the X044 lower-dimensional actual-centre block. Uniform immediate-defect escape, the class-V ordinal, full global termination, and arbitrary-dimensional positive-characteristic resolution remain open.
