# X056 Supplement: Saturated Kummer-Monoid Terminalization

After simultaneous row reduction and Cartier peeling, the terminal radicial envelope is controlled by a finite saturated Kummer map of fs monoids

```text
P -> Q=(P + eta_j | p^(e_j) eta_j=b_j)^sat.
```

The normalization is the Kummer monoid chart together with a regular logarithmic Jacobian block. A common projective regular subdivision of the labelled cone complex is implemented by ordinary blowups in regular boundary strata. The theorem depends only on the saturated monoid and therefore is independent of root generators and tower order.
