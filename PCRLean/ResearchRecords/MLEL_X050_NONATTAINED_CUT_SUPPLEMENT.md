# MLEL-X050 Supplement

## Nonattained Artin--Schreier Cuts and the Uniform Escape Problem

**Parent:** MLEL-X050 / BGI-FSP-NCE-FINAL  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** exact frontier compression; Uniform Nonattained-Cut Escape remains open.

---

## 1. The cut

Let `L/K` be a nontrivial Artin--Schreier degree-p extension of a henselian valued field of characteristic `p`. For a defining right-hand side `f`, put `q(f)=v(f)`. Define the lower cut

```text
Delta(L/K)={gamma in Gamma_Q | gamma<q(f) for some defining f}.
```

The cut is independent of the chosen generator. It is attained exactly when a best generator exists, hence exactly when the extension is defectless.

For a defect extension, choose a cofinal family `f_i` with strictly increasing qualities. Replacing this family by a cofinal tail does not change the cut.

---

## 2. Finite-state blindness

A finite state uses only finitely many representatives and finitely many coefficient, Hasse, differential and ramification shadows. Let `H_i` be the ideal generated by the reciprocals of the representatives enrolled by stage `i`.

Each strict coherent improvement enlarges a contraction of `H_i` or a related shadow module in a fixed Noetherian ancestor. Such enlargements are finite. After stabilization, better generators still exist but are invisible to the frozen coherent state.

Thus no rank factoring through a fixed finite state can count all improvements of a genuine defect cut.

---

## 3. Dependent and independent branches

A dependent Artin--Schreier defect layer is connected to a purely inseparable immediate extension and is routed to the radicial/Frobenius branch.

The independent branch retains a stable nonattained cut not explained by a radicial shadow. It is the genuine class-V frontier.

---

## 4. Uniform Nonattained-Cut Escape

The required theorem is:

> Let a stable independent Artin--Schreier cut be represented on a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood, together with a finite prepared event-visible state, a finite common model and immutable projection/source ancestry. There exists a finite portfolio of ordinary regular jointly legal base words and finite reprojections such that every valuation follows one branch and every nonterminal leaf either:
>
> - admits a best generator;
> - becomes dependent/radicial;
> - changes the cut or its pro-isomorphism/convex-subgroup class;
> - lowers effective key degree, conductor height, valuation support dimension or common-model class;
> - produces a new nonzero coherent contraction and re-enters finite-state preparation.

No branch may use the number of approximants, a cofinal-tail shift or the defect of a fixed projection as progress.

---

## 5. Most likely geometric route

The maximum-likelihood route is to combine:

1. a finite tame--wild degree-p reprojection portfolio;
2. the full best-generator ideal `H(L/K)` and its logarithmic differential/trace companions;
3. the independent/dependent classification of Artin--Schreier defect;
4. the finite chain of isolated convex subgroups of a finite-rank valuation;
5. X049 base-first Norm--Rees preparation for every coherent contraction;
6. X045 no-upward birth and multiset descent.

At a stable independent cut, either a reprojection exposes a coherent shadow, the associated isolated subgroup strictly drops, or the extension enters a defectless/dependent chamber. Proving this trichotomy uniformly is the remaining effectivity theorem.

---

## 6. Truth boundary

The existence of the cut, its nonattainment in the defect case and the finite-state blindness theorem are rigorous. The uniform actual-centre/reprojection escape is not proved in the current baseline.
