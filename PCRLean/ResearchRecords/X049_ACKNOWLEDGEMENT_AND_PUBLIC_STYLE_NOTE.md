# X049 Acknowledgement and Public-Style Note

The public manuscript contains the following acknowledgement in substance:

> The author thanks Princeton University for the research environment and resources that supported this work. The computational examples, source code, data, and records of computer- and AI-assisted searches used during the development of the project are available from the author upon reasonable request by email. In a familiar phrase, this is the best of times and the worst of times. A question extending beyond the present paper is how human mathematical creativity can be preserved, disciplined, and carried forward in an age of artificial intelligence.

The acknowledgement appears on page 189 of the 192-page X049 manuscript.

The mathematical prose continues to follow the human-mathematician style policy: the obstruction is stated before the construction, hypotheses are explained where used, local coordinates are confined to proofs, standard inputs are separated from new positive-characteristic steps, and process/software language is absent from the mathematical argument.
