> characteristic `p>0`, let `W` be a smooth finite-type `k`-scheme, let
> `X <= W` be a reduced closed subscheme, and let `E` be an SNC boundary.
> There is a finite sequence of ordinary blowups along actual coherent

> the total boundary.  The final strict transform is regular and SNC with the
> total boundary.  The construction commutes with smooth morphisms and perfect
> ground-field extensions in the stated category.
