# Projected series abstract

We construct a functorial embedded resolution algorithm for reduced schemes of
finite type over a perfect field of positive characteristic.  A marked
singularity is encoded by a finite intrinsic Frobenius-Hasse state carrying
active and passive owners, an ordered boundary, and fixed-ancestry source and
debt data.  Its affine conormal equations define a cokernel obstruction whose
content and projectivity discriminants yield graph, hybrid, unit,
Schur-Fitting and radicial chambers.

Nonunique permissible closures are retained as a finite intersection
arrangement.  Clean arrangements are serialized by a symmetry-compatible
wonderful building set.  A nonregular intersection with singular reduced
support is handled by lower-dimensional relative resolution.  If the reduced
support is regular, anchor-contact ideals reduce the residual nonreduced
intersection to lower-dimensional principalization followed by ambient contact
cleaning.

For each raw centre, the associated graded modules of all passive owners are
compressed into finitely many low pieces and coherent sheaves on the
projectivized normal bundle.  Proper flatification on the centre is dominated
by a lower-dimensional regular word and lifted ambiently; a parallel
log-conormal preparation enforces SNC compatibility.  Controlled transforms
preserve the complete finite state on every chart and overlap without reset.
A composite well-founded rank eliminates births, exceptional recharge,
kangaroo recurrence, singular Fitting kernels and immediate defect.  Descent
and symmetry-compatible finite serialization produce a global sequence of
ordinary blowups, yielding principalization, embedded resolution and
nonembedded resolution.
