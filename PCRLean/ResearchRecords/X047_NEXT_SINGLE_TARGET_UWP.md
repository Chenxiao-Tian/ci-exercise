# X047 Single-Target Focus Freeze

## Uniform Wild Preparation (UWP)

All further work on X047 is frozen to this theorem until it is proved, refuted, or split by a genuine geometric counterexample.

### Exact target

After finite-flat preparation of one degree-p wild layer, construct on the regular upper model a finite word of ordinary blowups in regular centres which simultaneously prepares the complete finite state: marked ideals, normal Hasse jets, differential and ramification modules, exactified mixed-Rees data, boundary, source filtration, relation data, and terminal certificates.

The word must be uniform on the certified quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood. Every chart must terminate, lower an earlier valuation or geometric coordinate, or remain in the same prepared chamber with a lower finite monomial profile. All ideals, Rees algebras and labels required for the complete Amitsur discrepancy must be retained.

### Already available after UWP

- finite discrepancy purification makes the upper word groupoid-compatible after finitely many downstairs blocks;
- zero-shadow descent produces a downstairs ordinary regular jointly legal word;
- the coherent and valuation recurrence constructions then remove the corresponding class-V token.

### Minimal proof cut

```text
UWP-1  finite simultaneous monomialization of the complete upper state;
UWP-2  regular ordinary upper centres;
UWP-3  uniformity on a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood;
UWP-4  all-chart strict decrease or terminality;
UWP-5  preservation of finite-flat relation and Amitsur data;
UWP-6  common-refinement and smooth/etale compatibility.
```

### Research discipline

Until UWP is resolved, do not introduce another global rank, source ledger, centre grammar, descent formalism, recurrent-class taxonomy, or manuscript architecture. Do not use alteration as the final resolution, and do not use an approximation index or cofinal-tail counter as progress.

### Immediate work order

1. Treat one affine degree-p wild layer on one regular upper local model.
2. Write the complete finite upper marked family.
3. Form the joint marked sum-of-powers aggregate and the finite monomial face complex.
4. Check every standard blowup chart and the finite-flat relation.
5. Prove a strict finite profile drop.
6. Only then pass to a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood and finite common refinements.

X047 remains `OPEN_GAP`; general positive-characteristic resolution is not established.
