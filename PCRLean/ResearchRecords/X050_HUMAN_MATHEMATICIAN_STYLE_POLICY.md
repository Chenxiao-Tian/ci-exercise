# X050 Human-Mathematician Style Policy

Future public manuscripts preserve all mathematical hypotheses, formulae, and proof dependencies while using the prose most likely to appear in a serious research article.

- Begin with the geometric or valuation-theoretic obstruction.
- State standard valuation theory separately from the new geometric construction.
- Explain the role of each hypothesis where it is used.
- Use coordinates only for local proofs and return to intrinsic statements.
- Place counterexamples next to the theorem boundary they enforce.
- Distinguish finite-state preparation from monomialization of the full extension.
- Avoid workflow, software, formalization, status, estimate, and promotional language.
- Keep only mathematically defined technical terms.

The X050 public manuscript was edited under this policy. In particular, the best-generator ideal and the local-monomialization counterexamples precede the cut-escape construction, and the acknowledgement contains only the Princeton/resource and materials-availability statement requested by the author.
