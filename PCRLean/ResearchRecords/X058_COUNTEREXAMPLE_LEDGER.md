# X058 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. Stagewise Cartier-crystal lengths are not comparable

A blowup changes the scheme, Cartier algebra, boundary, and transform functor. Finite length on each stage separately does not produce one birational rank. X058 transports all objects to one constructible category on the initial model.

## 2. Perverse sheaves are not assumed semisimple

The proof keeps extension classes. No decomposition theorem is invoked. Finite length means finite Jordan--Holder length, not canonical splitting.

## 3. Intermediate extension does not choose a centre

Intermediate extension gives a canonical global identity to a simple event factor. The centre is still defined by algebraic Fitting supports and actual ideals in the Cartier--Rees state.

## 4. Chart restrictions are aliases

One primitive local system may appear on several blowup charts or chart components. These restrictions have the same intermediate extension and are one source identity, not several births.

## 5. Nilpotent Cartier differences are not events

Presentation changes, cleaning, and some chartwise discrepancies may add locally nilpotent Cartier modules. They are zero in the crystal category and are not charged.

## 6. A new chamber cannot reset the categorical coordinate

The global rank is lexicographic. A change of chamber is allowed only after an earlier geometric coordinate has strictly decreased. At fixed earlier rank, all event factors remain in the same constructible ancestor category.

## 7. Constructible support alone is not an actual centre

A perverse support may be singular or fail active/passive/logarithmic legality. The full coloured actual-centre theorem remains necessary.

## 8. Kummer normalization is not the final modification

Relative Kummer normalization and normalized blowups are comparison devices. The public resolution sequence consists only of ordinary blowups in coherent regular centres.

## 9. External validation boundary

The public paper is unconditional under the user's X058 assumption. The constructible transform-ancestor theorem, algebraic--constructible no-reset theorem, full Cartier--Spencer--Kummer descent, and universal termination theorem have not been independently peer reviewed or completely formalized in this archive.
