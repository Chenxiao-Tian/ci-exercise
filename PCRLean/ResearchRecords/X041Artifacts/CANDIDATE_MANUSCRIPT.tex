\begin{abstract}
Let $C$ be a regular carrier in a regular ambient scheme and let
$G=\operatorname{gr}_C(M)$ be the complete normal associated-graded module of a
passive owner.  We introduce a canonical proper compactification of the entire
affine normal-cone module by total-degree homogenization.  If
$S=\operatorname{Sym}(I_C/I_C^2)$, then $G[z]$ is a finite graded module over
$S[z]$, the standard open $D_+(z)$ in $\operatorname{Proj}S[z]$ is the full
affine normal cone, and flatness of the associated projective sheaf is
equivalent to flatness of $G$.  Functorial flatification of proper coherent
sheaves therefore yields canonical source words without a presentation-
dependent Rees--Serre cutoff.

We also isolate a standard cofinality theorem: the iterated strict transform of
a centre word equals the strict transform for its distinguished composite word
ideal, finite products give common refinements, and once a source transform is
flat every later strict transform is its pullback.  The remaining
project-specific theorem is an all-chart Regular-Flag Flat-Lift statement
transporting the complete normal module through nested regular blowups.  The
chapter records the exact candidate theorem and its open interfaces; it does
not prove resolution in positive characteristic.
\end{abstract}

\section{The homogenized normal packet}
Let $C\hookrightarrow X$ be a regular immersion with ideal $I$ and conormal
bundle $E=I/I^2$.  Put
\[
 S=\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathcal O_C}(E).
\]
For a coherent owner $M$ set
\[
 G=\operatorname{gr}_I(M)=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}I^nM/I^{n+1}M.
\]
The module $G$ is finite and graded over $S$.  Let $z$ have degree one and
define the total-degree homogenization
\[
 \mathsf H(G)=G\otimes_{\mathcal O_C}\mathcal O_C[z],
 \qquad
 \mathsf H(G)_d=\bigoplus_{0\leq n\leq d}G_nz^{d-n}.
\]
It is finite over
\[
 \overline S=S[z]=\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathcal O_C}(\mathcal O_C\oplus E).
\]
Let
\[
 \overline N_C=\operatorname{Proj}_C(\overline S)
 =\mathbf P_C(\mathcal O_C\oplus E),
 \qquad
 \overline{\mathcal G}=\widetilde{\mathsf H(G)}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Affine recovery, candidate scheme interface]
There are canonical identifications
\[
 D_+(z)\cong\operatorname{Spec}_C(S),
 \qquad
 \overline{\mathcal G}|_{D_+(z)}\cong\widetilde G.
\]
They commute with flat base change on $C$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Algebraic calculation]
The degree-zero parts after localizing at $z$ are
\[
 (S[z]_z)_0=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}S_nz^{-n}\cong S
\]
and
\[
 (\mathsf H(G)_z)_0=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}G_nz^{-n}\cong G.
\]
The relative sheaf and base-change statements require the standard Proj
interfaces.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Flatness equivalence, candidate scheme interface]
The following are equivalent:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $G$ is flat over $C$;
\item every homogeneous piece $G_n$ is flat over $C$;
\item $\mathsf H(G)$ is flat over $C$;
\item $\overline{\mathcal G}$ is flat over $C$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Proof architecture]
The module $G$ is the direct sum of its homogeneous pieces, so (1) and (2) are
equivalent.  As an $\mathcal O_C$-module,
$\mathsf H(G)$ is the direct sum of the finite triangular slices
$\bigoplus_{n\leq d}G_n$, giving (2)$\Leftrightarrow$(3).  Flatness of
$\mathsf H(G)$ implies flatness of its associated sheaf.  Conversely, restriction
of a flat projective sheaf to $D_+(z)$ gives the flat affine module $G$.
\end{proof}

The proposition retains every normal degree and avoids an arbitrary Serre
cutoff.  It is also presentation independent because it is defined from the
canonical graded module rather than a chosen finite presentation.

\section{Functorial flatifier sources}
For a finite passive portfolio take the direct sum of the homogenized sheaves.
Logarithmic and source modules may be included on proper identity components.
The resulting finite disjoint-union morphism is proper over $C$.  Rydh's
functorial flatification theorem for coherent sheaves on proper noetherian
morphisms yields a sequence of blowups, supported outside the maximal flat
open, whose strict transform is flat and whose formation is functorial under
flat base change.

In positive characteristic the flatifier centres need not be regular.  The
sequence is therefore source data for lower-dimensional principalization, not
the final ambient resolution word.

\section{Centre-exact word compression}
Strict transform depends on the centre.  Nevertheless, a finite centre word
has a distinguished composite blowup ideal produced by the composition theorem
for blowups.  Its centre support records the set-theoretic union of the word
centres.  For this distinguished ideal, strict-transform transitivity gives
\[
 \operatorname{ST}_{J^{\mathrm{word}}}(F)
 \cong
 \operatorname{ST}_{J_r}\cdots\operatorname{ST}_{J_1}(F).
\]
This does not hold for an arbitrary ideal defining an isomorphic modification;
Cartier changes require the corresponding torsion-free hypothesis.

Let $J_\lambda^{\mathrm{word}}$ be the finite orbit of carrier source ideals and
let $K_\lambda$ be their ambient traces.  Put
\[
 K_{\mathrm{com}}=\prod_\lambda K_\lambda.
\]
The blowup of $K_{\mathrm{com}}$ factors through every source modification.  If
$F_\lambda^{\mathrm{flat}}$ is the flat terminal transform of source
$\lambda$, then its strict transform on the common refinement is simply its
pullback.  This is the same product-ideal and strict-transform-transitivity
argument used in the local-to-global flatification theorem.

\section{Lower-dimensional realization}
Every maximal carrier $C$ has dimension below that of the ambient scheme.  The
flatifier source is componentwise admissible, hence supported away from every
generic point of $C$.  Apply the strengthened relative principalization theorem
in lower dimension to $K_{\mathrm{com}}|_C$, enrolling the complete active,
normal, logarithmic, contact, source, and debt portfolio.  The output is a
finite word of regular centres on the successive carrier transforms.  By the
universal property, the word factors through the common flatifier.

Active marked legality lifts ambiently by ideal-power monotonicity.  Passive and
logarithmic legality are supplied by the following theorem.

\section{Regular-Flag Flat-Lift}
Let $D\subset C\subset X$ be regular immersions.  Etale-locally choose a regular
sequence
\[
 I_C=(x_1,\ldots,x_r),\qquad
 I_D=(x_1,\ldots,x_r,y_1,\ldots,y_s).
\]
Only the $y_j$-pivot charts of $\operatorname{Bl}_D(X)$ meet the strict transform
$C'$.  On the $y_1$-chart,
\[
 x_i=y_1x_i',\qquad y_j=y_1y_j'\quad(j>1),
\]
and $I_{C'}=(x_1',\ldots,x_r')$.

\begin{theorem}[Regular-Flag Flat-Lift, target]
Let $M$ be a coherent passive owner.  Assume:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\operatorname{gr}_C(M)$ is flat over $C$;
\item the Rees filtration sequences of $M$ are Tor-safe for the blowup in $D$;
\item $D$ is normally flat for the complete induced carrier portfolio;
\item the flag is compatible with the total SNC boundary.
\end{enumerate}
Let $M'$ and $C'$ be strict transforms after blowing up $X$ in $D$.  Then for
every $n\geq0$ there is a canonical all-chart isomorphism
\[
 \frac{I_{C'}^nM'}{I_{C'}^{n+1}M'}
 \cong
 \operatorname{pr}^*\left(\frac{I_C^nM}{I_C^{n+1}M}\right)
 \otimes\mathcal O_{C'}(nE_C),
\]
with the sign determined by the convention $x_i=y_1x_i'$.  The isomorphisms
agree on overlaps, commute with smooth base change, and preserve source and
owner lineage.  Consequently $\operatorname{gr}_{C'}(M')$ and its homogenized
projective completion are flat over $C'$.
\end{theorem}

The theorem is open.  Its final flatness conclusion follows formally once the
degreewise isomorphisms are constructed.

\section{The candidate global compiler}
The local architecture is
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{canonical homogenized normal packet}
\longrightarrow
\text{functorial source words}
\longrightarrow
\text{centre-exact common product}
\longrightarrow
\text{lower-dimensional regular realization}
\longrightarrow
\text{Regular-Flag Flat-Lift}.
\end{gathered}
\]
After source-arrangement completion, deepest-first wonderful serialization has
only disjoint and nested interactions.  Hironaka normal-flatness transitivity
prepares every stratum; the theorem above handles the nested blowups.  A
support/source/flag/word rank controls the internal macro, while the existing
source/debt/SCC rank controls outer execution.

\begin{theorem}[Homogenized normal-cone carrier legalization, target]
Under strong relative principalization in dimensions below $n$, every finite
symmetry-stable arrangement of raw regular carriers in a smooth $n$-dimensional
ambient scheme admits a finite centre-exact common-refinement word whose
centres are ordinary, regular, nonidentity, active-permissible, passive-normal-
flat and Tor-safe, and SNC-compatible.  The complete finite state reenters on
all charts without reset, and every nonterminal macro strictly lowers the
global well-founded rank.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
This is a maximum-likelihood theorem target.  The relative-Proj flatness
interface, lower-dimensional full-portfolio realization, Regular-Flag
Flat-Lift, hereditary reconstruction, and termination handoff remain open.
General resolution in positive characteristic is not asserted.
\end{remark}
