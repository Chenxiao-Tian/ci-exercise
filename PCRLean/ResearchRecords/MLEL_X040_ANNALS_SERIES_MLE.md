## Epistemic boundary

This is a maximum-likelihood publication architecture, not a claim that
arbitrary-dimensional positive-characteristic resolution has been proved.  The
series becomes unconditional only after every load-bearing claim, dependency
edge, actual centre, chart, overlap, source-word comparison, naturality law,
no-reset identity, and strict descent certificate is closed.

X040 makes three corrections.

1. Independent maximal-carrier flatifier words are not ambiently closed because
   a preparatory centre for one carrier can cross another carrier.
2. An arbitrary choice of one flatifier per carrier is not made functorial by
   symmetrizing the chosen ideals afterwards.  A finite proper Rees-Serre packet
   and functorial flatification of proper morphisms must supply the carrier
   source words.
3. A finite flatifier word may be represented by one ideal only as a
   modification morphism.  Its strict-transform semantics remain attached to
   the complete centre-enriched source-word package.

The ambient modification ideals of the functorial source packages are
multiplied.  Their product blowup is a common refinement, its support has lower
dimension than the maximal carriers, and a full-portfolio relative
principalization in lower dimension is expected to realize it by ordinary
regular jointly legal centres.  Source-arrangement completion then reduces the
remaining wonderful interactions to the disjoint and nested cases.
