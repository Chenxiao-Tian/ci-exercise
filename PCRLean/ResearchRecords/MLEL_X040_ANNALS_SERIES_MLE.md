## Epistemic boundary

This document estimates the form of an eventual correct proof.  It does not
assert that arbitrary-dimensional resolution in positive characteristic has
been proved.  The series becomes unconditional only after every load-bearing
claim, edge theorem, actual centre, standard chart, overlap, naturality law,
no-reset identity, and strict descent certificate is closed.

The final X040 architecture makes two corrections.  First, independent
maximal-carrier flatification words are replaced by one symmetry-invariant
product of ambient trace ideals, whose blowup is a common refinement.  Second,
the individual flatifier words must themselves be functorial: the complete
affine normal-cone packet is retained as the exact all-degree object, while a
finite projective Rees-Serre packet and functorial flatification of proper
morphisms supply canonical source words compatible with smooth and étale base
change.  The common product support has lower dimension than the maximal
carriers and is realized by the strong relative principalization theorem in
lower dimension.  Source arrangement completion reduces later interactions to
the disjoint or nested cases.

# Projected series abstract

We construct a functorial embedded resolution algorithm for reduced schemes of
finite type over a perfect field of positive characteristic.  A marked
singularity is encoded by a finite intrinsic Frobenius-Hasse state carrying
active and passive owners, an ordered boundary, and fixed-ancestry source and
debt data.  Its affine conormal equations define a cokernel obstruction whose
content and projectivity discriminants yield graph, hybrid, unit,
Schur-Fitting, and radicial chambers.

Nonunique permissible closures are retained as a finite intersection
arrangement.  Every maximal raw carrier carries an affine normal-cone packet and
a finite proper Rees-Serre packet.  Functorial proper flatification of the
latter yields equivariant centre-enriched source words.  Their ambient traces
are multiplied to form a canonical common-refinement ideal.  Because every
source flatifier is componentwise admissible, the support of this product has
lower dimension.  A relative principalization word in regular jointly legal
centres realizes the common modification and factors through every flatifier,
simultaneously making all maximal carriers normally flat.

The source-enriched intersection completion is resolved in lower support
dimension until it is clean.  A deepest-first wonderful word then has only
disjoint and nested interactions.  Hironaka's tensor theorem propagates normal
flatness to every stratum; the Regular-Flag Flat-Lift theorem transports it
through nested blowups, and flatness kills all exceptional purification
torsion.  All standard charts and overlaps preserve the complete finite state
without reset.

A composite well-founded rank eliminates common-flatifier support, contact,
births, exceptional debt, kangaroo recurrence, singular Fitting kernels, and
immediate defect.  Descent and symmetry-compatible finite serialization
produce a global sequence of ordinary blowups, yielding principalization,
embedded resolution, and nonembedded resolution.
