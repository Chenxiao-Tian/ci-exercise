## 0. Executive verdict

The first X047 round proved that a finite upper centre word descends whenever its complete Cech--Rees shadow vanishes. The remaining problem was to make a useful upper word Cech-compatible. The present round separates this into two parts and closes the descent-purification part.

It includes comparison, inverse, cocycle, ideal-embedding, multiplication, grading, source, boundary, transform and terminal-certificate defects.
2. If the discrepancy is nonzero, its first intrinsic cohomology/Fitting layer is a coherent proper colour downstairs. Enrol this colour, together with the complete upper packet and the relation data, in the X044 full portfolio and apply the lower-dimensional actual-centre block.
3. Exactification identifies the successor discrepancy with the controlled transform of the old discrepancy plus a new event lying in the fixed X045 ancestral event envelope. Hence every purification round either lowers an earlier geometric profile or strictly enlarges the consumed submodule of one Noetherian Amitsur envelope.
4. Noetherian ACC and the earlier well-founded profiles imply that only finitely many discrepancy-purification rounds occur. At the last round the complete Cech--Rees shadow is zero.
5. The zero-shadow theorem then descends the complete upper state and the whole finite centre word stage by stage. Thus **groupoid-compatible descent is conditionally closed for every given finite upper monomial word**.
6. Cech-Compatible Wild Monomialization consequently reduces to one geometric theorem: construct, uniformly on a quasi-compact independent Artin--Schreier neighbourhood, a finite upper ordinary word which monomializes the complete event-visible state. The remaining single target is therefore **Uniform Wild Preparation (UWP)**, not another descent theorem and not another source or rank formalism. Under UWP, the X047 purification theorem gives UDE-EV; X045 then supplies the class-V elimination and the global source-causal rank.

## 4. Groupoid discrepancy purification

### Theorem 4.1 -- finite discrepancy-purification theorem

Assume:

1. the X044 actual-centre theorem for every coherent discrepancy colour;
2. X042--X043 exactified transform and all-chart reentry;
3. X045 ancestral no-upward birth for the complete coherent grammar;
4. after each downstairs preparation, the complete upper state admits a finite monomial word in the same wild-layer chamber or an earlier geometric profile drops.

Then, after finitely many downstairs ordinary centre blocks, there is a finite upper monomial word whose complete Amitsur discrepancy is zero. This word descends uniquely to an ordinary regular jointly legal word on the original model.

### Proof

At each round compute the complete discrepancy. If it is zero, apply the first X047 zero-shadow theorem. If it is nonzero, take the first layer `L_Am` and apply its downstairs actual-centre block. Exactified hereditary transport yields the successor discrepancy. Either an earlier well-founded profile drops, or the process remains in the fixed chamber and Theorem 3.1 strictly enlarges the consumed submodule. The lexicographic product of the earlier profile and the foundation rank of the consumed submodule is well founded. Hence the iteration is finite. At the last round the discrepancy is zero and the word descends stage by stage.

### Corollary 4.2 -- conditional Cech-compatible wild monomialization

If a finite upper monomialization word exists uniformly for the complete event-visible state on a quasi-compact wild neighbourhood, then a finite downstairs ordinary word exists and is jointly legal. No further descent hypothesis is required.
