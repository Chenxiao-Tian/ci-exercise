# Projected final series abstract

We construct a functorial embedded resolution algorithm for reduced schemes of
finite type over a perfect field of positive characteristic.  A marked
singularity is encoded by a finite intrinsic Frobenius–Hasse state carrying
active and passive owners, an ordered boundary, and fixed-ancestry source and
debt data.  Its affine conormal equations define a cokernel obstruction whose
content and projectivity discriminants yield graph, hybrid, unit,
Schur–Fitting, and radicial chambers.

Nonunique permissible closures form a finite intersection arrangement and are
serialized by a symmetry-compatible wonderful building set.  Regular-support
nonreduced intersections are represented by anchor-contact ideals on regular
lower-dimensional carriers.  Passive normal flatness is represented by finite
projective-normal packets on projectivized normal bundles.  Every carrier ideal
retains its source and pure-transform payload; residual Cartier centres are
executed as ambient trace steps even when their carrier blowups are identities.

The central transform theorem describes the failure of purification and normal
associated grading to commute.  The failure is a finite graded-torsion packet
supported on the exceptional boundary, together with a finite twisted Rees
base-change defect.  Vanishing gives exact projective-normal transport;
nonvanishing gives divisorial lower-dimensional descent.  Controlled
Cartier-trace macros preserve all charts, overlaps, owners, sources, debts,
contacts, and boundary identities without reset.  A composite well-founded
rank eliminates interchange defects, contact, births, exceptional recharge,
kangaroo recurrence, singular Fitting kernels, and immediate defect.  Descent
and symmetry-compatible finite serialization produce a global sequence of
ordinary blowups, yielding principalization, embedded resolution, and
nonembedded resolution.

This abstract is a maximum-likelihood reconstruction, not a claim of
completion.
