# X065 Supplement: All-Pivot Projective Carrier Descent

## Fixed-carrier firewall

A coefficient-transform identity on charts meeting the strict transform of a chosen carrier does not control normal-pivot charts. In positive characteristic the maximal locus may move to precisely those charts. Carrier disappearance is therefore not progress.

## Projective replacement

For every candidate centre, retain the full homogeneous normal-Hasse state on the projectivized normal bundle. On tangential charts the coefficient state is the ordinary controlled transform. On a normal-pivot chart the state is the dehomogenized degree-mark initial form on the exceptional projective face.

The carrier rank is the finite multiset

```text
(carrier dimension, Hasse level, projective face depth,
 Fitting profile, generic length, primitive ancestry).
```

Raw exceptional names are absent.

## Local formulas

For a tangential pivot `u_1`,

```text
D_[y']^[alpha](f')|_(H')
  = u_1^(-(b-|alpha|)) a_alpha(u_1,u_1u').
```

For a normal pivot `y_1`, the restriction to the exceptional divisor is the dehomogenization of

```text
Phi_C(f)=sum_(|alpha|+|beta|=b) a_(alpha,beta) U^beta Y^alpha.
```

Thus charts outside the old strict transform remain inside one finite projective state.

## Strictness

The Projective Hasse maximum theorem makes maximal strata regular by explicit determinantal pivots and Hasse detection of singular maximum strata. After the centre block, a surviving carrier uses ordinary coefficient descent; a lost carrier is replaced only by a proper projective/Fitting face or by a strictly shorter generic quotient. Hence all charts are paid.

## Induction meaning

Only a surviving smooth carrier licenses a lower ambient-dimensional call. Normal-pivot charts remain same-dimensional but lower the projective carrier cycle. This is the exact substitute for persistent maximal contact used by X065.
