# X048 Human-Mathematician Style Policy

Future public manuscripts preserve the mathematics exactly while using the most likely prose of a research mathematician.

- Begin with the ramification obstruction, not with the name of a construction.
- State local algebra intrinsically after giving the coordinate proof.
- Separate standard finite-flat algebra and toric geometry from the genuinely new wild-effectivity theorem.
- Put the example `t=s^e, J=(s)` next to the false universal descent claim it refutes.
- Explain why alteration is diagnostic and why the final modification is an ordinary base word.
- Avoid protocol labels, status walls, software language, promotional adjectives, repeated roadmaps, and formulaic summaries.
- Retain technical terms only after they have been defined as mathematical objects.

The X048 public article was edited under this policy without changing its intended hypotheses, formulae, or dependency relations.
