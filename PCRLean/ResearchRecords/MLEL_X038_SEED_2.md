For `A=gr_L(N)`, `H=gr_L^ind(H^0_(u)(N))`, and `U=H^0_(u)(A)`, the expected exact sequence is

```text
0 -> U/H -> gr_L(N/H^0_(u)(N)) -> A/U -> 0.
```

Moreover `U/H = H^0_(u)(gr_L(N/H^0_(u)(N)))`. The defect is therefore a single exceptional graded-torsion kernel, rather than an arbitrary kernel/cokernel pair.
