# MLEL-X057 Supplement

## Transform-Complete Ancestor Cartier Crystals

### Fixed-category problem

Finite length of Cartier crystals is a theorem on a fixed F-finite scheme. Successive blowups change the scheme and Cartier algebra. Therefore the numbers `length C(Sigma_i)` are not, by themselves, a birational rank.

### Ancestor construction

Freeze every geometric coordinate preceding the crystal coordinate and let `W_N` be the first model in that chamber. The complete finite state is generated by a fixed finite exactified grammar. Applying this grammar to the ancestor presentations gives a bounded coherent Cartier crystal `U_N`.

For every later stage, push the intrinsic state and event crystals to `W_N`. Centre-exact Rees descent, universal exactification, derived direct image for Cartier crystals, and the event octahedron show that all coherent cohomology crystals are subquotients of `U_N`.

### Consumed subcrystal

Let `D_i` be generated by all primitive factors already charged before stage `i`. Chart aliases and inherited returns remain in `D_i`; locally nilpotent differences vanish in the crystal category. A genuinely new event gives a nonzero factor of `U_N/D_i` and therefore a strict inclusion

```text
D_i proper_subset D_(i+1).
```

The residual labelled Jordan--Holder multiset of `U_N/D_i` strictly decreases. Since `U_N` has finite length, the chamber contains only finitely many nonterminal blocks.

### Relation to valuative certificates

A defectless projection attached to a witness valuation produces a quotient of `U_N/D_i`, not an unrelated crystal on each stage. This fixed-category quotient is the exact object used in the termination contradiction.
