# MLEL-X060 Supplement

## Derived Gluing, Canonical Support Quotients, and Finite Monodromy

### 1. Derived gluing envelope

Proper transport is kept as a bounded perverse Postnikov tower. Equal perverse cohomology objects do not determine a derived object, so every Postnikov k-invariant, recollement gluing morphism, and connecting map is retained. The finitely many intermediate-extension simple factors of the complete state generate finite-dimensional Ext spaces over `F_p`. A genuinely new gluing class strictly enlarges a consumed subspace of one fixed Ext space.

### 2. Canonical support quotients

For every irreducible support `S`, quotient the perverse heart by objects supported properly inside `S`. The image is the generic support layer of `S`. Maximal active supports form a finite antichain. The actual-centre construction treats the whole antichain at once, so no auxiliary total ordering of incomparable strata enters the algorithm or its smooth-pullback comparison.

### 3. Finite monodromy hull

A finite stratification alone does not bound local-system types. X060 proves that the finite exactified operation grammar, together with uniform presentation-rank and Postnikov-amplitude bounds, factors all residual local systems through one finite quotient on each stratum. The finite strata, finite monodromy groups, bounded ranks, and bounded Ext degrees form one finite representation envelope.

### 4. Cartier-dual matching

The Cartier--Riemann--Hilbert correspondence is contravariant. A leading perverse kernel corresponds to a Cartier quotient, not a Cartier submodule. A coherent representative of the quotient has the same maximal support as the perverse kernel; its maximal Fitting support is enrolled in the complete actual-centre portfolio. Hence the constructible factor counted by the rank and the coherent factor removed by the blowup are the same event in opposite categories.

### 5. Combined strict alternative

Every nonterminal complete block satisfies one of:

```text
an earlier geometric coordinate decreases;
the normalized leading perverse degree decreases;
the maximal support antichain decreases;
a nonzero residual finite-length quotient is consumed;
a residual Postnikov/recollement Ext coordinate is consumed.
```

All coordinates are fixed-category, finite, or well founded, and no coordinate resets under chart changes or common refinements.
