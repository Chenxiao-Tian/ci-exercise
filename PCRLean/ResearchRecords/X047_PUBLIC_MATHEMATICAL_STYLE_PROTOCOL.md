# X047 Public Mathematical Style Protocol

This file governs the public paper only. It does not change mathematical statements, hypotheses, notation, or proof dependencies.

## 1. Voice and structure

- Write as a conventional research article in algebraic geometry.
- State the geometric problem before introducing notation.
- Use definitions, lemmas, propositions and theorems only when they carry mathematical work.
- Let proofs explain the decisive idea, the reduction, and the exact point at which each hypothesis is used.
- Avoid a sequence of short managerial sentences when one connected mathematical paragraph is clearer.

## 2. Terminology

Prefer ordinary mathematical terms:

```text
finite system / family / complex        rather than packet, when no ambiguity results;
construction / procedure                rather than compiler;
finite sequence of blowups              rather than word, except where centre-word semantics matter;
auxiliary module or marked component    rather than owner, except in a fixed technical definition;
provenance filtration                   rather than ledger in public prose;
comparison defect                       rather than budget or debt, unless the defined invariant is meant.
```

Technical terms such as Frobenius--Hasse system, mixed-Rees module, event complex, source filtration and centre-exact sequence may be retained after precise definition.

## 3. Exposition

- Do not announce that an argument is rigorous, complete, canonical, exact, or standard unless the following sentences show why.
- Do not repeat the whole proof architecture at the beginning of every section.
- Use examples and counterexamples near the statement they constrain.
- Separate geometric constructions from the well-foundedness argument.
- Distinguish a theorem used from the literature from a new theorem proved in the paper.
- Keep the alteration role explicit: it is an auxiliary descent device, never the final resolution.

## 4. Public-language exclusions

The public manuscript does not mention research prompts, language models, formalization branches, confidence estimates, posterior selection, CI, repository state, or internal node labels. These remain in the private research record.

## 6. Final edit test

A paragraph passes the style test when a specialist could encounter it in an ordinary research journal without needing to know the history of the project, while every hypothesis and mathematical dependency remains visible.
