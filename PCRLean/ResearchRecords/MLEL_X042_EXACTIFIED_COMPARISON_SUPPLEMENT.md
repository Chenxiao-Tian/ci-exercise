# MLEL-X042 Supplement

## Universal Exactification of the Tor-Valabrega Comparison

**Parent:** MLEL-X042 / RFG-TVC  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** carrier-side exactness closure; ambient successor identification open.

### 1. Problem corrected

Flatifying only the three defect sheaves

\[
\ker\alpha_n,\qquad \ker\beta_n,\qquad \operatorname{coker}\beta_n
\]

does not by itself imply that their strict transforms are the defects of the successor comparison maps. Nonflat base change can change kernels and images.

### 2. Exactified portfolio

Retain the finite all-degree portfolio

\[
A_n,C_n,B_n,
\ker\alpha_n,\operatorname{im}\alpha_n,
\ker\beta_n,\operatorname{im}\beta_n,
\operatorname{coker}\beta_n,
\]

together with every multiplication-image sequence. After homogenization this is one proper coherent finite portfolio.

### 3. Universal exactness lemma

If every retained module is flat over the prepared carrier, then the exact sequences

\[
0\to\ker\alpha_n\to A_n\to\operatorname{im}\alpha_n\to0,
\]
\[
0\to\ker\beta_n\to C_n\to\operatorname{im}\beta_n\to0,
\]
\[
0\to\operatorname{im}\beta_n\to B_n\to\operatorname{coker}\beta_n\to0
\]

remain exact after arbitrary base change. This follows from vanishing of the relevant `Tor_1` groups. Consequently kernels, images, and cokernels commute with all later centre-exact pullbacks.

### 4. Flat-kill

The three defect modules are generically zero. Functorial/admissible flatification of the complete exactified portfolio makes them flat; on a reduced Noetherian carrier, a flat module vanishing at every minimal prime is zero. Therefore `alpha_n` and `beta_n` become isomorphisms simultaneously in every degree and for every owner, and remain so after later centre-exact base change.

### 5. Remaining edge

The still-open geometric theorem is:

> the pullback of the prepared universal mixed-Rees comparison diagram along the regular ambient trace word is canonically the comparison diagram of the actual successor carrier and actual controlled strict transforms, with the exceptional line twist and all source/boundary labels.

Thus X042 moves the frontier from abstract kernel stability to ambient realization and chart identification.
