# 1. Two X034 bridges that collapse to standard algebraic geometry

## 1.1 Regular-intersection collapse

Let `X` be a regular locally Noetherian scheme and let `{Z_i}` be finitely many
regular closed subschemes.  Assume every nonempty scheme-theoretic intersection

```text
Z_S = intersection_{i in S} Z_i
```

is regular.

At a point of `Z_S`, write `R=O_{X,x}` and let `K_S` be the ideal of `Z_S`.
Both `R` and `R/K_S` are regular local.  Hence `K_S` is generated by a regular
sequence; in particular `Z_S -> X` is a regular immersion and
`K_S/K_S^2` is finite locally free.

There is a canonical surjection

```text
directSum_i (I_i/I_i^2)|_{Z_S} -> K_S/K_S^2.
```

Its source and target are finite locally free.  The target is projective, so
the surjection splits and its kernel is finite locally free.  This kernel is
the excess conormal bundle.  It may be nonzero: nested regular centres and
centres sharing normal directions are clean excess intersections.

Dualizing the conormal sequence gives the required tangent-space equality.
Because regular immersions, conormal sequences, finite locally free modules and
splittings are local and stable under etale base change, the construction is
etale-natural.  Thus the scheme-level mathematical content of X034-G1/G2 is a
standard theorem once every intersection stratum is known to be regular.

The project-specific work begins after this collapse: active, passive, boundary
and nonidentity certificates must still be proved for the same strata.

## 1.2 Projectivity discriminant

Let `M` be a finitely presented module over a Noetherian ring `R`.  Put

```text
F_r = Fitt_r(M),     F_{-1}=0,
Delta_r(M) = F_r * Ann(F_{r-1}).
```

For a prime `p`, Fitting ideals commute with localization and the usual rank
criterion gives

```text
M_p free of rank r
<-> (F_{r-1})_p = 0 and (F_r)_p = R_p.
```

For a finitely generated ideal `I`,

```text
I_p = 0  <->  Ann(I) is not contained in p.
```

Also

```text
(F_r)_p = R_p  <->  F_r is not contained in p.
```

Since `p` is prime, it avoids `F_r*Ann(F_{r-1})` exactly when it avoids both
factors.  Therefore

```text
D(Delta_r(M)) = { p | M_p is free of rank r }.
```

If `M` is generated by `n` elements, then

```text
Delta_proj(M) = sum_{r=0}^n Delta_r(M)
```

has

```text
D(Delta_proj(M)) = { p | M_p is finite projective }.
```

This proves the mathematical point-locus theorem proposed in X034.  It does not
replace the scheme-theoretic Fitting stratification and it does not itself
construct a permissible centre on the closed discriminant.

# 2. Why pure support-dimension descent is insufficient

The most tempting first-defect argument is:

```text
bad stratum Z is a proper closed subscheme
-> dim Z < dim X
-> invoke induction on dimension.
```

This is insufficient because a bad scheme can have regular reduced support.
For

```text
R = k[x,y],
I=(y),
J=(y-x^m),
K=I+J=(y,x^m),
```

we have

```text
Z = Spec(R/K) = Spec(k[x]/(x^m)),
Z_red = Spec(k),
```

so the reduced support is already regular and zero-dimensional.  Nevertheless
one needs a nontrivial blowup process, and in the standard chart the contact
length changes from `m` to `m-1`.

The correct recursion must distinguish singularity of the reduced carrier from
nilpotent thickness over a regular carrier.

# 3. The support-singular branch

Let `Z` be a bad stratum in a regular ambient scheme `X`, and set `Y=Z_red`.
Assume `Y` is singular.

Because `Z` is a proper intersection stratum, `dim Y < dim X`.  The resolution
program in dimension `< dim X`, applied relative to the restrictions of the
owner portfolio and boundary, should produce a finite word

```text
Y_n -> ... -> Y_0=Y
```

of ordinary blowups along regular centres `C_i subset Y_i`.

Blow up the successive ambient transforms `X_i` along the same closed centres.
The strict transform theorem identifies the transform of `Y_i` inside `X_i`
with the blowup of `Y_i` in `C_i`.  Thus the lower-dimensional word is not only
a formal guide: it is literally the strict-transform word inside the ambient
scheme.

If `X_i` and `C_i` are regular, the ambient blowup remains regular.  Moreover,
if an active owner ideal `D` satisfies

```text
D <= I_{Y_i}^m
```

and `C_i subset Y_i`, then `I_{Y_i} <= I_{C_i}` and hence

```text
D <= I_{C_i}^m.
```

Active marked permissibility is therefore inherited automatically by every
relative centre.  This exact ideal-power argument is formalized in
`StratumInductionActiveLift.lean`.

What does **not** follow automatically is equally important:

```text
passive Tor safety,
normal flatness,
SNC compatibility with the ambient boundary,
nonidentity,
owner/source/debt no-reset.
```

These are the new ambient-lift edge obligations.  They cannot be imported from
a bare resolution of `Y`; the lower-dimensional recursive call must be a
relative resolution problem carrying the full restricted portfolio.

# 4. The regular-support thickness branch

Assume `Y=Z_red` is regular while `Z` is nonregular.  Let

```text
K = I_Z,      J = I_Y = radical(K).
```

The ideal `J/K` in `O_Z` is nilpotent.  The canonical finite graded object is

```text
Theta(Z/Y)
  = gr_{J/K}(O_Z)_{>0}
  = directSum_{q>=1} (J^q+K)/(J^{q+1}+K).
```

We call `Theta(Z/Y)` the **nilpotent contact packet** or **thickness cone**.
It is the positive-degree part of the normal cone of the reduced carrier `Y`
in the thickened scheme `Z`.

Basic properties expected from the definition are:

1. `Theta(Z/Y)=0` if and only if `Z=Y`;
2. it is finite graded over `O_Y`;
3. it is intrinsic and presentation independent;
4. under flat maps preserving geometric reducedness, it obeys exact base
   change;
5. its largest nonzero degree is a finite thickness index;
6. the supports and Fitting ideals of its graded pieces give a finite
   stratification of the maximal-thickness locus.

The elementary certificate

```text
J^e <= K
```

means the nilpotent carrier is killed at level `e`.  Monotonicity and the
level-one collapse are formalized in `NilpotentThickness.lean`.

The maximum-likelihood strict-descent theorem is:

```text
NILPOTENT-CONTACT REDUCTION
On a regular carrier Y, a nonzero finite thickness packet admits a finite
relative ordinary-centre word such that on every successor chart either

  (a) the maximal thickness degree drops, or
  (b) at fixed degree, the support dimension of the top graded piece drops, or
  (c) at fixed support, the Hasse/contact profile drops, or
  (d) at fixed Hasse data, the Schur-Fitting/kernel profile drops.
```

Projectivity discriminants control the locally free chambers of the graded
pieces.  Positive Schur pivots lower presentation shape.  Rank-zero pieces enter
Frobenius-root or radicial descent.  This is the precise role of the existing
Hasse, content-ideal, Schur-Fitting and DFK-KR machinery in the first-defect
branch.

# 5. Choice-free bad layers

A functorial algorithm must not choose one first bad stratum from a symmetry
orbit.  For a finite intersection arrangement and a geometric gate `Good`,
define

```text
Bad = { S | not Good(S) }.
```

The exact finite coverage is

```text
Bad = empty  <->  every stratum is good,
Bad nonempty <-> some bad stratum exists.
```

The bad layer is then partitioned intrinsically into

```text
Bad_sing = { S in Bad | (S_red is singular) },
Bad_thick = { S in Bad | (S_red is regular) }.
```

The two layers are disjoint and their union is the full bad layer.  Lean source
for this exact coverage is in

```text
FiniteBadStratumCoverage.lean
SupportThicknessAlternative.lean.
```

Geometrically one processes intrinsic maximal-rank or minimal-depth layers,
never a chosen point.  This preserves the symmetry-safe philosophy of X032-X034.
