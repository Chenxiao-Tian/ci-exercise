## 2. Projectivized contact height

Let `K` be a thick intersection ideal, `J=rad(K)`, and

```text
L=(K+J^2)/J^2 subset J/J^2,
Q=(J/J^2)/L.
```

The residual initial ideal in `Sym(Q)` defines finite cumulative coefficient ideals on `P(Q)`. The smallest degree at which the coefficient ideal becomes the unit ideal is the projective contact height. It is frame-free and upper semicontinuous.

In a standard chart with surviving projective direction, exceptional division shifts every residual normal degree by one. Hence positive height changes by

```text
h -> h-1.
```

If the residual projective direction disappears, the branch exits to a lower support or a Fitting/radicial chamber. This refines the support--thickness dichotomy: dimension handles singular reduced support, while projective contact height handles nilpotent thickness over a regular support.

## 3. Exact joint marked aggregate

For a finite family `{(a_lambda,b_lambda)}`, choose a common multiple `B` and put `c_lambda=B/b_lambda`. Define

```text
A_joint=sum_lambda a_lambda^c_lambda.
```

If `D` is a regular centre with ideal `J_D`, then `gr_(J_D)(O_W)` is reduced. Therefore

```text
ord_(J_D)(f^m)=m ord_(J_D)(f),
```

and

```text
A_joint subset J_D^B
iff
a_lambda subset J_D^b_lambda for all lambda.
```

For a permissible blowup with exceptional ideal `I_H`,

```text
A_joint'
 = I_H^(-B) A_joint O_(W')
 = sum_lambda (a_lambda')^c_lambda.
```

The aggregate is therefore an exact one-pair encoding of simultaneous permissibility on regular centres. Colours remain separate in the portfolio; only the centre gate is aggregated.
