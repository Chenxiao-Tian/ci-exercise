## 1. Canonical Morita algebra and frame independence

\[
\mathscr E_{B/A}=\operatorname{End}_A(B).
\]

On a local Frobenius frame, multiplication and finite Hasse operators generate all matrix units by Pascal separation; hence they generate \(\mathscr E_{B/A}\). A change of frame conjugates the local generating family inside the same intrinsic algebra. An ideal stable under the full operator algebra is extended from A, and this statement holds degreewise for its powers. Consequently the entire Rees algebra and the centre-exact blowup descend.

The X057 manuscript does not treat a selected Frobenius basis as part of the invariant. Frames are local proof devices; the Morita algebra, the stable ideal, and the descended Rees algebra are the geometric objects.
