```text
C_J(I_*) = in_J(K) / sum_a in_J(I_a).
```

In graph coordinates, blowing up the regular reduced carrier divides every

The new central candidate theorem is the **Rees-exact contact-cancellation

# 1. Finite projective-tail flatness criterion

Let `A` be Noetherian, let

```text
B = directSum_{n>=0} B_n
```

be a standard graded `A`-algebra of finite type, let

```text
pi : P = Proj_A(B) -> Spec(A),
```

and let `G` be a finite graded `B`-module, zero below a finite lower degree
`d`.  Write `F=G~`.

## Theorem 1.1 -- finite projective-tail criterion

There is an integer `N>=d` such that

```text
G is A-flat
<->
F is A-flat and G_n is A-flat for d <= n < N.
```

### Proof architecture

For a finite graded module, Serre's comparison theorem gives an integer `N_1`
for which

```text
G_n -> pi_* F(n)
```

is an isomorphism for every `n>=N_1`.  Relative Serre vanishing gives `N_2`
such that higher direct images of `F(n)` vanish for `n>=N_2`.  If `F` is flat
over `A`, cohomology and base change and constancy of the Hilbert polynomial on
connected components imply that `pi_*F(n)` is finite locally free for all
sufficiently large `n`.  Choose `N` above these bounds.

If `G` is `A`-flat, every graded piece is an `A`-direct summand and is flat.
On every standard projective chart, the degree-zero part of a homogeneous
localization is a direct summand of a filtered colimit of copies of `G`, so
`F` is flat.

Conversely, the assumed finite window is flat and the projective comparison
makes every degree `n>=N` finite locally free.  Hence every graded piece is
flat.  Their direct sum `G` is flat.

The inherited cutoff remains valid after flat base change.  It is a certificate
and need not be recomputed minimally on a successor.



# 4. Projective conormal chamber and residual contact ideal

Assume the degree-one conormal map has constant projective rank.  Each regular
immersion `Y -> Z_a -> X` has a linear normal cone, so `in_J(I_a)` is generated
by its degree-one subbundle `L_a`.  Let

```text
E = J/J^2,
L = sum_a L_a,
T = E/L,
S = Sym_A(T).
```

On this chamber,

```text
sum_a in_J(I_a) = L * B,
Q = in_J(K)/(L*B) <= S,
gr_J(R/K) = S/Q.
```

Because `radical(K)=J`, some power of the irrelevant ideal of `S` lies in `Q`.
Thus `S/Q` is finite graded over `A` and `Q` is fiberwise irrelevant-primary.
The positive thickness cone and the contact ideal are equivalent descriptions,
but `Q` retains the linear owner decomposition needed for the blowup law.

The exactness of initial ideals and of `C_J(I_*)` under flat base change is a
Rees-module Tor statement.  The finite Rees--Serre packets of Section 2 are the
proposed finite certificates for this exactness.

# 5. Directional contact-depth flag

For `r>=1`, let `Q_{<=r}` be the homogeneous ideal generated by the pieces
`Q_1,...,Q_r`, and define

```text
F_r(Q) = Proj(S/Q_{<=r}) <= P(T).
```

The closed subsets form a descending finite flag

```text
P(T)=F_1(Q) >= F_2(Q) >= ... >= F_k(Q)=empty
```

for some finite `k`.  At a geometric direction `v` of `P(T)`, define

```text
delta_Q(v)
  = min { r : some q in Q_r has q(v) != 0 }.
```

Then

```text
v in F_r(Q) <-> delta_Q(v) > r,
```

and the global projective contact depth is

```text
kappa(Q) = max_v delta_Q(v)
         = min { r : F_r(Q)=empty }.
```

This flag is finite, intrinsic, owner-aware, and compatible with every
symmetry preserving the filtered family.

# 6. Split graph blowup theorem

Work formally or etale-locally on a split graph chamber.  After choosing a
reference centre, write the regular centres as

```text
I_0 = (z_1,...,z_s),
I_a = (z_mu - phi_{a,mu}(x))_mu,
```

where every `phi_{a,mu}` has `x`-order at least two.  The common tangent
quotient is generated by the `x` variables, and `Q` is the initial ideal of the
differences `phi_{a,mu}-phi_{0,mu}`.

Blow up the reduced carrier `(x,z)=0`.  In the chart `x_1=t`, write

```text
x_i=t*u_i,
z_mu=t*w_mu.
```

Every regular-centre equation has order one and its strict transform is
obtained by dividing by `t`:

```text
w_mu - phi_{a,mu}(t,t*u)/t.
```

If the first nonzero homogeneous cancellation coefficient at a direction `v`
has degree `d`, then

```text
phi(t,t*u)/t = t^(d-1) * (nonzero coefficient at v) + higher t-order.
```

Hence the local exponent of the transformed intersection along the exceptional
carrier is exactly

```text
delta_Q(v)-1.
```

Equivalently, the locus on the exceptional projective bundle where the new
exponent is at least `r` is precisely `F_r(Q)`.

Therefore one blowup has only two strict outcomes:

1. at fixed common tangent rank, every surviving directional contact depth is
   shifted down by one; or
2. a residual equation becomes linear and is absorbed into `L`, strictly
   lowering `rank(T)`.

This gives the well-founded lexicographic rank

```text
(rank(T), kappa(Q)).
```

The calculation is characteristic-free.  In characteristic `p`, a residual
term `x^p` still becomes an exceptional term of order `p-1`; no ordinary
Jacobian derivative is required.

The fully global theorem still requires etale descent of the graph chamber,
all standard charts, overlap equality, regularization of the maximal-depth
locus, and hereditary reconstruction of the packet.
