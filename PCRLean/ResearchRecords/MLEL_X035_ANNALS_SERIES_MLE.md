## Paper I - 90 pages

### Intrinsic Frobenius-Hasse States, Affine Conormal Packets, and Projectivity Discriminants

The paper constructs the finite intrinsic state, proves the restricted
Hasse-Morita descent theorem in its exact scope, packetizes Noetherian operator
orbits, builds the affine conormal obstruction, identifies the intrinsic order
ideal with content on finite-projective chambers, and proves the projectivity
discriminant formula

```text
Delta_r(M)=Fitt_r(M)*Ann(Fitt_{r-1}(M)).
```

Its final interface is a finite presentation divided into projective
content-ideal chambers and closed Schur-Fitting residual chambers.

## Paper II - 122 pages

### Content Ideals, Support-Thickness Strata, and Wonderful Actual-Centre Synthesis

The paper treats graph, proper-hybrid, unit, Fitting and rank-zero chambers.  It
proves the minimizer-space no-selector theorem, constructs the full finite
intersection arrangement, establishes regular-intersection collapse and
projective excess bundles, and introduces the support-thickness alternative.
The support-singular layer invokes lower-dimensional relative resolution; the
regular-support layer carries the nilpotent contact packet.  Clean arrangements
are serialized by the maximal wonderful building set.  The output is a
universal actual-centre-word certificate.

## Paper III - 118 pages

### Joint Legality, Ambient Lifting, All-Chart Transforms, and Hereditary Reentry without Reset

The paper proves that both wonderful words and support-thickness words lift to
one ambient ordinary blowup sequence satisfying, simultaneously, active
marked-power containment, passive Tor safety, normal flatness, SNC boundary
compatibility and nonidentity.  It computes every standard chart and overlap,
controls exceptional debt, and proves no reset under cleaning, Hasse saturation,
normalization and integral closure.  Its output is the hereditary successor
compiler.

## Paper IV - 94 pages
