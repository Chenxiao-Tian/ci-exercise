## 1. The best-generator ideal

Let `A` be a henselian valuation ring of characteristic `p>0`, with fraction field `K`. Let `L/K` be a nontrivial Artin--Schreier extension of degree `p`. Put

```text
A_AS(L/K)
  = {f in K^* | T^p-T-f defines L/K}.
```

Any two elements of this set differ, up to multiplication by an element of `F_p^*`, by `h^p-h` for some `h in K`.

### Definition 1.1 -- best-generator ideal

Define

```text
H_(L/K) = (1/f | f in A_AS(L/K)) subset A.
```

The inclusion in `A` follows after choosing the usual nontrivial representatives with nonpositive valuation.

### Theorem 1.2 -- finite-generation criterion

The following conditions are equivalent.

```text
(a) H_(L/K) is finitely generated;
(b) H_(L/K) is principal;
(c) L/K admits a best Artin--Schreier generator;
(d) L/K is defectless.
```

### Proof

Every finitely generated ideal of a valuation ring is generated by an element of least valuation among a finite generating set. Thus `(a)` implies `(b)`. If `H=(1/f_1,...,1/f_m)`, choose `1/f_i` with least valuation. Then `f_i` has maximal valuation among the defining right-hand sides and generates the whole ideal; any strictly better right-hand side would contribute an element of strictly smaller valuation to `H`, a contradiction. Hence `f_i` is best and `(b)` implies `(c)`. If `f_best` is best, every other `1/f` has valuation at least `v(1/f_best)`, so `H=(1/f_best)`; thus `(c)` implies `(b)`. The equivalence of `(c)` and `(d)` is the Artin--Schreier best-`f` theorem for henselian valuation rings.

### Corollary 1.3 -- noncoherent defect carrier

If `L/K` has defect, then `H_(L/K)` is not finitely generated. In particular, no coherent ideal on any finite Noetherian model can pull back exactly to `H_(L/K)` in the valuation ring.

### Proof

A coherent ideal on a Noetherian model is finitely generated, and its extension to a valuation ring is principal. Apply Theorem 1.2.

## 2. The improvement cut

For `f in A_AS(L/K)` define its quality by `q(f)=v(f)`. A generator `f'` is better than `f` when `q(f')>q(f)`. Define the lower cut

```text
Delta_(L/K)
  = {gamma in Gamma_Q | gamma < q(f) for some f in A_AS(L/K)}.
```

Equivalently, one may use the distance cut of an Artin--Schreier generator from `K`; the two descriptions carry the same nonattainment phenomenon.

### Proposition 2.1

The cut `Delta_(L/K)` is attained if and only if a best generator exists. Thus it is attained exactly in the defectless case.

For a genuine defect extension, every finite list of representatives can be improved. A tail shift in a cofinal list of improvements does not change the cut or the associated pro-object.

### Consequence 2.2 -- no finite-stage cut rank

None of the following is an intrinsic strict rank:

```text
number of Artin--Schreier improvements already used;
index in one chosen key-polynomial or generator sequence;
cofinal-tail shift;
maximum quality among one fixed finite list;
defect factor of one fixed valued-field projection.
```

A valid escape must attain the cut, change the cut or its pro-isomorphism class, or lower an independent valuation/geometric coordinate.

## 3. Coherent approximants and visible improvement events

For a finite subset `F={f_1,...,f_m}` of `A_AS(L/K)`, define

```text
H_F=(1/f_1,...,1/f_m) subset H_(L/K).
```

Fix one finite common Noetherian model `X_A` and contract the finite ideal and all associated coefficient, Hasse, differential, conductor, source, and Norm--Rees data to the ancestor coloured algebra.

### Definition 3.1 -- visible improvement

A better generator `g` is visible on `X_A` if adjoining `1/g` strictly enlarges the contracted ancestor ideal or one of the associated exactified shadow modules. Otherwise it is invisible at that finite state.

### Theorem 3.2 -- visible-improvement ACC

While the earlier geometric, wild-layer, presentation, and common-model profiles remain fixed, only finitely many visible improvements occur.

### Proof

All visible improvement layers lie in one finite ancestral event envelope. A genuinely new layer strictly enlarges its consumed submodule or the contraction of `H_F`. These are submodules of a Noetherian module. Hence the chains stabilize.

### Corollary 3.3

An infinite defect branch has a tail on which every coherent event-visible shadow is stable. Every later Artin--Schreier improvement changes only the nonattained cut and is invisible to the frozen finite state.

## 4. Full monomialization is the wrong target

A universal theorem asserting that every finite wild extension becomes monomial after birational modifications of regular source and target is false in positive characteristic. Defect examples show failure even for weak local monomialization.

### Definition 4.1 -- finite-state wild preparation

A finite-state preparation of a class-`V` packet is a finite sequence of ordinary blowups which prepares only the finite coloured state used by the resolution algorithm:

```text
marked ideals and normal Hasse jets;
projectivity, conormal and Fitting modules;
exactified Tor--Valabrega diagrams;
differential, trace, conductor and ramification shadows;
source, boundary, contact and history filtrations;
Norm--Rees and finite descent-gap data;
all comparison maps needed for the next state.
```

It does not require the full finite algebra map or the valued-field extension to be monomial.

### Theorem 4.2 -- conditional finite-state preparation

Assume the X044 lower-dimensional actual-centre theorem and the X042--X043 transform/reentry theorem. Every finite event-visible state admits a finite base-first preparation in which each coherent ideal is invertible or monomial, each finite module is flat on its rank stratum, every defining exact sequence is universally exact, and all boundary and source data are compatible with the resulting SNC boundary.

### Proof

Apply the exact joint marked aggregate to the active ideals, the global Norm--Rees construction to divisorial wild shadows, and simultaneous flatification to the complete exactified module portfolio. Enrol every flatifying and overlap defect in the full coloured centre construction. Lower-dimensional preparation realizes the resulting modifications by ordinary regular jointly legal centres. The all-chart transform theorem reconstructs the finite state without reset.

### Remark 4.3

This theorem prepares a finite list of observables. It does not turn an independent defect extension into a monomial extension and therefore does not conflict with the failure of local monomialization.

Assume semantic completeness of the finite coherent colour grammar. After finitely many coherent preparation and event-purification blocks, every nonterminal class-`V` leaf has:

```text

all visible improvement ideals stabilized;
```

### Proof

## 6. Uniform Nonattained-Cut Escape

The remaining theorem is the following.

> **UNCE.** Let an independent Artin--Schreier defect packet be represented on a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood by a stable finite prepared state and a nonattained improvement cut. There exists a finite portfolio of ordinary regular jointly legal base words and finite reprojections such that every valuation follows one branch and every nonterminal leaf satisfies one of:
>
> 1. a best generator exists, so the layer is defectless;
> 2. the layer becomes dependent and enters the radicial branch;
> 3. the improvement cut or its convex-subgroup/pro-isomorphism class changes;
> 4. effective key degree, conductor height, valuation-support dimension, or common-model class strictly drops;
> 5. a previously invisible improvement acquires a nonzero coherent contraction and re-enters finite-state preparation.

The action must be uniform on the whole certified neighbourhood, preserve immutable ancestry, and create no equal-or-higher class-`V` token.

### Maximum-likelihood proof route

The most likely proof uses the finite tame--wild degree-`p` tower, the complete ramification ideal `H_(L/K)`, logarithmic differential and trace comparisons, and a finite reprojection portfolio. On each projection, either a best representative is obtained, a coherent contraction of `H` or the differential-ramification packet becomes effective, or the distance cut is transported to a strictly lower isolated convex subgroup. Quasi-compactness gives finitely many branches. The rank is the lexicographic product of the finite convex-subgroup height, effective key degree, conductor/support profile, the Noetherian coherent-shadow rank, and the finite projection portfolio height.

This route remains conjectural at the effectivity step for an arbitrary stable independent cut.
