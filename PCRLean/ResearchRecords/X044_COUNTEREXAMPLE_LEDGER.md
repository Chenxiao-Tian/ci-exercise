# X044 Counterexample and Boundary Ledger

## CMR-1: uncoloured product loses owner semantics

Two marked ideals can have the same product after forgetting colours while carrying different marks and source labels. The coloured multi-Rees grading is retained throughout common modification and toroidal preparation.

## CMR-2: radicals lose thickness

The ideals `(y,x^m)` have the same reduced support for all `m` but different contact lengths. The positive nilpotent graded module and its Hasse/Fitting coefficient colours remain in the portfolio.

## CMR-3: Fitting support alone does not detect the normal cone

Projectivity of `I/I^2` does not imply that `Sym(I/I^2) -> gr_I(O_W)` is an isomorphism. The homogeneous relation ideal is a separate colour.

## CMR-4: principalizing one selected minimizer breaks symmetry

A symmetry groupoid can permute cardinal-minimal correction families without fixing one. X044 retains the full finite minimizer and intersection arrangement and processes complete symmetry orbits.

## CMR-5: fixed normal jets are insufficient

The finite normal-jet cutoff is the actual mark `b`. No uniform cutoff independent of the marks controls all coefficient packets.

## CMR-6: flatifying defects without exactification is unstable

After a nonflat preparation, old kernels and images need not pull back to new kernels and images. X042 sources, targets, images, kernels, cokernels, and multiplication-image sequences remain separate colours.

## CMR-7: stacky or weighted toroidal principalization is not the final word

Stacky or weighted monomialization may guide combinatorics, but the target theorem permits only ordinary blowups in regular scheme centres. X044 uses ordinary boundary-stratum blowups.

## CMR-8: finite centre blocks do not imply global termination

The source-join semilattice bounds births inside one block only. Successor Frobenius--Hasse packets can create new coherent event cohomology. Recurrent-class exit and the global rank remain separate open nodes.

## CMR-9: product aggregation does not express joint permissibility

A product adds orders. One colour with excess order can compensate for another below its mark, so product containment does not imply ownerwise containment. The common-mark sum of powers is used instead; its reverse implication relies on the reduced associated graded ring of a regular centre.

## CMR-10: coherent event cones do not imply ancestral termination

A bounded coherent event cone gives finitely many new packets at one transition, but the same ancestor can reappear through infinitely many future transitions. Ancestral no-repetition and recurrent-class exit remain load bearing.
