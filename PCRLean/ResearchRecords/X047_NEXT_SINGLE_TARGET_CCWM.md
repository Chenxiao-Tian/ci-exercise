# X047 Single-Target Focus Freeze

## Cech-Compatible Wild Monomialization (CCWM)

All further work on the X047 line is frozen to this theorem until it is proved, refuted, or decomposed by a genuine counterexample.

### Exact target

Let one independent Artin--Schreier degree-p layer be represented after finite-flat preparation by:

- a finite faithfully flat cover `f:Y->X`;
- one quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood;
- immutable valued-field, projection, layer and source ancestry;
- the complete event-visible coefficient, Hasse, differential-ramification, source, boundary and Cech-Rees portfolio;
- a regular prepared upper model.

Construct a finite word of ordinary blowups on `Y` which monomializes the complete upper packet and such that, after finite constructible refinement, one of the following holds uniformly:

1. the complete Cech-Rees shadow is zero, so the word descends stagewise to `X` by X047;
2. the first nonzero Cech/differential shadow is a coherent proper colour on `X`, and its X044 source-closed actual-centre block strictly lowers the complete shadow profile on every valuation and chart;
3. an earlier valuation, key-degree, conductor, support, Frobenius/radicial, prime-layer, or common-model coordinate strictly drops.

### Minimal proof cut

```text
CCWM-1  a finite upper monomialization word for the complete packet;
CCWM-2  flat-base-change naturality of the upper construction on the Cech nerve;
CCWM-3  finite coherence of the stagewise Rees/cocycle discrepancy;
CCWM-4  strict reduction of the first nonzero discrepancy by a downstairs block;
CCWM-5  preservation of valuation ancestry and absence of equal/higher class-V birth;
CCWM-6  common-refinement and smooth/etale compatibility.
```

### Forbidden diversions

Until CCWM is resolved, do not introduce:

- another global invariant;
- another source ledger;
- another centre grammar;
- another broad recurrent-class classification;
- another manuscript architecture;
- alteration as the final resolution;
- group invariance as a substitute for Cech descent;
- trace or differential vanishing as a substitute for a descent datum;
- a finite approximation index or cofinal-tail counter.

### Immediate work order

1. Work on one affine finite-flat Artin--Schreier model `A->B`.
2. Choose one finite upper monomial ideal/centre word from the prepared packet.
3. Compute `p_0^*J`, `p_1^*J`, the Rees discrepancy, and the cocycle discrepancy.
4. Prove zero discrepancy implies ideal and word descent.
5. For nonzero discrepancy, test whether its first Fitting ideal is strictly changed by the descended X044 block.
6. Only after this single-stage theorem is stable may a finite word and finite p-layer tower be compiled.

The X047 line remains `OPEN_GAP`; general positive-characteristic resolution is not established.
