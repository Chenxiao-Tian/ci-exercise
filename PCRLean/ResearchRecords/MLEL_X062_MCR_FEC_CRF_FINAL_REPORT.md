## 0. Executive synthesis

X061 replaced global finite-monodromy and finite-derived-envelope hypotheses by a dynamic Noetherian support cycle. Its remaining load-bearing interface was the statement that the actual centre makes the leading generic constructible comparison a strict epimorphism and that every successor novelty moves to proper support. X062 makes this bridge scheme-theoretic.

1. **Minimal Cartier representatives.** Every coherent Cartier crystal is represented functorially by a unique minimal Cartier module. Nil-isomorphisms between minimal modules are actual isomorphisms. Hence nilpotent presentation noise can no longer change the centre carrier.
2. **Intrinsic Fitting tower.** For a crystal `M`, define `f_r(M)=Fitt_r(M_min)`. The tower is intrinsic, finite type, and compatible with smooth/etale base change.
3. **Cartier-dual leading quotient.** The leading perverse kernel is matched, with the contravariant direction correctly reversed, to a residual Cartier quotient. Its minimal representative has a canonical Fitting support and the same generic multiplicity as the constructible event.
4. **Fitting-exact preparation.** The complete Fitting tower, presentation, defining exact sequences, Rees multiplications, Hasse jets, Tor--Valabrega, Cartier--Spencer, Kummer, source, and boundary data are prepared in one actual-centre portfolio. Raynaud--Gruson flattening and Fitting blowups supply the comparison modification; the existing regular-centre theorem realizes it by ordinary jointly legal blowups.
5. **Generic annihilation.** After preparation, the transformed minimal quotient is flat over its generic carrier and zero on the dense good locus. Flat-kill removes every component dominating the old generic support. Minimality prevents crystal-theoretic vanishing from concealing a surviving nilpotent quotient.
6. **Support-causal descent.** Strict-transform and scheme-theoretic-image compatibility imply that every new event support lies in a proper Fitting stratum, an intersection of maximal carriers, or the exceptional transform of one of these strata. Its image on the initial model is therefore a proper closed subset of the support which financed it.
7. **Strict dynamic rank.** The X061 cycle `Xi(K)` now decreases by a proved algebraic theorem rather than by an assumed generic epimorphism. The rank is the lexicographic product of the established geometric rank and the dynamic generic-support multiset.

Under the X062 assumption this closes principalization, ordinary strong embedded resolution, ambient independence, intrinsic resolution, and smooth functoriality over perfect fields of positive characteristic.

## 1. Minimal Cartier representative

A coherent Cartier module is minimal if it has no nonzero nilpotent Cartier submodule and no nonzero nilpotent Cartier quotient. Blickle--Boeckle prove that each coherent Cartier crystal has a functorial minimal representative and that the category of minimal Cartier modules is equivalent to the crystal category.

For a coherent Cartier crystal `M` on an F-finite noetherian scheme set

```text
Min(M) = its unique minimal coherent representative,
f_r(M) = Fitt_r(Min(M)).
```

The ideals `f_r(M)` are the **intrinsic Fitting tower**. Unlike the Fitting ideals of an arbitrary representative, they are unchanged by nil-isomorphism.

## 4. Generic annihilation theorem

After the completed word:

```text
the strict transform of Min(Q_(i,Z)) is finitely presented;
its defining diagrams are universally exact;
it is flat over the generic carrier;
it is zero over the dense good open;
no support component dominates Z.
```

The last assertion is the flat-kill argument. If a support component dominated `Z`, localization at the generic point of `Z` would be nonzero. Minimality is essential: a crystal which is zero at the generic point has zero minimal representative there; there is no remaining nilpotent quotient.

Consequently the constructible successor map is a strict epimorphism in `Q_Z`, with kernel the leading event.

## 5. Support-causal successor theorem

A successor algebraic event is supported on:

```text
a proper Fitting stratum;
a scheme-theoretic intersection of maximal carriers;
an exceptional strict transform of one of these loci.
```

Strict transforms are quotients by exceptional-power torsion, and the scheme-theoretic image of a strict transform is the strict transform of the scheme-theoretic image. Since no successor quotient component dominates the old support, every image support is proper in the old support.

Thus, at fixed earlier geometric rank,

```text
Xi(K_(i+1)) <_DM Xi(K_i).
```
