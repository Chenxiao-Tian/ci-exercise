# MLEL-X038 seed

Candidate title: Purification–Grading Interchange, Exceptional Rees Defects, and Divisorial Descent.

The local algebra studies a finite module `N`, a carrier ideal `L`, an exceptional Cartier parameter `u`, the exceptional torsion `T=H^0_(u)(N)`, and the induced inclusion

```text
gr_L^ind(T) <= H^0_(u)(gr_L(N)).
```

The quotient is the exceptional purification–grading defect. Its support lies on the exceptional divisor. Artin–Rees is expected to make it a finite graded packet.
