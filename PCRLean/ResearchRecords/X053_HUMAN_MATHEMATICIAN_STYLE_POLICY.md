# X053 Human-Mathematician Style Policy

The public manuscript preserves every mathematical hypothesis, formula, and dependency while using conventional research prose.

- State the obstruction before the construction.
- Separate standard valuation topology from the new common-refinement theorem.
- Use coordinates only in local proofs and state final results intrinsically.
- Place the singular-common-blowup counterexample beside the compactness theorem it limits.
- Do not attribute regularization of a common model to quasi-compactness.
- State pointwise DPE, hereditary common refinement, and functorial projection-atlas selection as separate logical inputs.
- Exclude workflow, software, formalization, prompt, and estimate language from the public article.
- Keep the first and final author blocks as `Princeton, NJ 08544, USA` with both email addresses; `Princeton University` occurs only in the acknowledgements.
