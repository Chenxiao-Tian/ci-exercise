# X056 Supplement: Base-First Norm-Rees Terminalization

Let `M subset M'` be a finite p-primary lattice extension and let the base fan be regular for `M`. The terminal normalized source is toroidal in `M'`.

Resolve the source fan by multiplicity-lowering star subdivisions. A source subdivision is the normalized blowup of a monomial ideal `J`. Its Ferrand norm ideal `I=N_pi(J)` is an intrinsic ideal on the base and satisfies, in every diagonal toroidal chart,

```text
integral_closure(I O_source)=integral_closure(J^[M':M]).
```

Retain the norm ideal, its Rees algebra, both descent-interval quotients, overlap comparisons, and all boundary/source labels as one exactified portfolio. Principalize this base portfolio by ordinary blowups in regular centres. The normalized pullback dominates the source star subdivision. The source cone-multiplicity multiset strictly decreases, so finite iteration makes the normalized source regular.

This replaces the need for an independently asserted fan simultaneously regular in two lattices. Normalization is used only for comparison; every modification of the original ambient scheme is an ordinary blowup in a regular centre.
