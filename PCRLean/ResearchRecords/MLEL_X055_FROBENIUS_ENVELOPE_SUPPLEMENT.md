## Intrinsic Frobenius Envelopes and Tower Independence

Let `B/A` be the normalization of a regular local ring in a finite purely inseparable extension. A root presentation is auxiliary. The intrinsic object is the normalized finite morphism together with the logarithmic cotangent complex and the finite differential-operator orbit of the marked state.

### Presentation comparison

Two presentations are placed in one common envelope by adjoining the union of their root generators. The logarithmic cotangent complexes pull back to the same complex. The finite Hasse operator algebras generate the same saturated endomorphism algebra by Pascal separation. Their stable ideals and coefficient modules therefore agree by Hasse-Morita descent.

The projective contact system is the projectivization of the same saturated normal coefficient module. The Fitting filtration is intrinsic. Actual centre ideals and source history descend through the common finite cover. Hence the state and its selected centre arrangement are independent of presentation.

### Tower comparison

A degree-p tower only orders generators of the same finite radicial envelope. The simultaneous state retains every active exponent and relation. One blowup acts on all relations at once. Thus permuting, refining, or shortening a tower changes neither the state nor the final centre word after empty centres are deleted.

### Base change

Localization, completion, and smooth base change commute with normalization in the selected branch, logarithmic cotangent complexes, finite Hasse packets, Fitting ideals, and the exactified comparison diagram. The construction is therefore local and smooth-functorial.
