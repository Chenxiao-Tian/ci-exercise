# X051 to X052 Supersession Note

X051's closed results are retained:

- the Artin--Schreier best-generator ideal for an independent defect layer is identified with the valuation prime attached to the canonical coarsening;
- this prime is idempotent and non-finitely generated;
- canonical coarsening reduces an idempotent distance `H^-` to primitive distance `0^-`;
- coarsening is classification rather than birational progress.

X052 supersedes only the proposed `PZGE` exit theorem. For a fixed rational subfield and fixed valued-field extension, ordinary blowups cannot change defect, best-generator existence, Artin--Schreier distance, or dependent/independent type. Once the finite coherent state has stabilized, a fixed-projection primitive token cannot be removed by model changes.

The replacement frontier is Defectless Projection Existence: change the rational subfield and prove that some admissible projection has defectless henselian branch. In the current architecture this is equivalent to local uniformization, conditional on the coherent centre and transform theorems.

General positive-characteristic resolution remains open.
