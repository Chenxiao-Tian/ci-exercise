# X057 Transform-Complete Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. Stagewise crystal length is not a birational rank

Finite length of Cartier crystals holds on one fixed F-finite scheme. Blowups change the scheme, Cartier algebra, boundary, strict-transform functor, and support. The list of stage lengths may increase or reset. X057 uses one fixed ancestor crystal per geometric chamber and a consumed subcrystal.

## 2. Nilpotent changes are not genuine events

Different presentations, Frobenius frames, exactified resolutions, or chart restrictions may add locally nilpotent Cartier modules. These vanish in the crystal category and cannot be charged as new factors.

## 3. A moving modulus may remain inside one simple crystal factor

Continuous changes of coefficient or direction data can occur without creating a new Jordan-Holder factor. Such movement is controlled by the earlier projective-contact, Frobenius, Fitting, and monomial coordinates; crystal length is used only after those coordinates are fixed.

## 4. Recreating a factor on another chart is not rebirth

All chart manifestations are pushed to the first model of the chamber and identified through the primitive source filtration. An inherited or sibling-chart return already lies in the consumed subcrystal.

## 5. A valuation-specific crystal cannot select the global centre

The defectless projection and valuation quotient are used only after an infinite intrinsic sequence is assumed. Centres are defined by the global Frobenius-Hasse and actual-centre state. Otherwise functoriality would fail.

## 6. Frobenius foliation straightening without Cartier data is unsound

In characteristic p, closed or involutive one-forms need not be exact; for example `x^(p-1) dx` has nonzero Cartier class. The Cartier-Spencer comparison and anchor data are indispensable.

## 7. A root lattice alone does not describe normalization

The positive monoid, faces, saturation, conductor, and hole module are required. X057 terminalization uses a fine saturated relative Kummer monoid and ordinary conductor-Fitting/Norm-Rees blowups.

## 8. Normalization is not the final modification

The Kummer normalization and normalized blowups are comparison objects. The delivered resolution sequence consists only of ordinary blowups in regular boundary-compatible centres.

## 9. External validation boundary

Under the user's X057 assumption the public article is unconditional. The transform-complete ancestor theorem, full Cartier-Spencer-Kummer descent, and universal valuative certificate theorem have not been independently peer reviewed or completely formalized in this archive.
