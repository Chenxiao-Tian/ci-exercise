# X046 Single-Target Focus Freeze

## Independent-Defect Effectivity (IDE)

All further work on the X046 line is frozen to one theorem until it is proved, refuted, or decomposed by a genuine counterexample.

### Exact theorem target

Let a stable independent prime-defect token be represented by:

- a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood;
- immutable valued-field, projection, prime-layer and source ancestry;
- one finite common model;
- the complete event-visible coefficient, Hasse, differential, trace, conductor, ramification and alteration-shadow portfolio;
- a monomialized packet on a finite uniformizing alteration portfolio.

Assume the complete finite coloured state is nonterminal throughout the neighbourhood. Prove that, after a finite constructible refinement, one of the following holds uniformly:

1. a nonzero proper coherent alteration shadow exists on the original model and its X044 source-closed actual-centre block strictly lowers the shadow profile;
2. effective key degree, Frobenius/radicial exponent, conductor height or valuation-support dimension strictly drops;
3. the finite projection becomes defectless or the valuation enters an Abhyankar/quasi-monomial chamber;
4. the prime-layer tower or common-model pro-isomorphism class changes;
5. all typed alteration shadows vanish and the monomial upper packet descends exactly, making the current token terminal.

### Minimal proof cut

```text
IDE-1  semantic completeness of the typed alteration-shadow complex;
IDE-2  first-nonzero-layer effectivity on the original model;
IDE-3  source-closed joint legality of the resulting centre block;
IDE-4  exact successor-shadow comparison on every valuation and chart;
IDE-5  zero-shadow exact descent from the monomial alteration packet;
IDE-6  no equal-or-higher independent-defect birth after the block.
```

### Forbidden diversions

Until IDE is resolved, do not create a new general invariant, new centre language, new source ledger, or another manuscript architecture. Do not use:

- a finite approximation index;
- a cofinal-tail shift;
- the defect factor of a fixed projection as a stage counter;
- alteration itself as the final resolution;
- one ramification ideal as a complete higher-degree detector;
- a nonprincipal valuation cut as a coherent ideal on a finite model.

### Immediate work order

1. Write the layerwise shadow comparison for a single independent Artin--Schreier prime layer.
2. Prove or falsify zero-shadow exact descent in that smallest chamber.
3. Extend only after the single-layer theorem is stable under common refinement and smooth/etale base change.
4. Then compile the finite prime tower by the octahedral filtration.

The X046 line remains `OPEN_GAP`; general positive-characteristic resolution is not established.
