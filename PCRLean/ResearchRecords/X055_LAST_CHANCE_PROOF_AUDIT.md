# X055 Last-Chance Proof Audit

## Audited vulnerability inherited from X054

The degree-p tower proof had two exposed interfaces:

1. dependence on a chosen tower and ordering of radicial generators;
2. terminal equations with a unit coefficient not shown to be removable.

## X055 repair

- The state is attached to the entire normalized finite radicial envelope.
- Different presentations are compared on a common Frobenius frame.
- Every blowup acts simultaneously on all root relations.
- On a smooth algebra over a perfect field, `ker(d)=R^p`; terminal differential-zero units are pth powers.
- The terminal normalization is described by one saturated root lattice.
- The toroidal resolution is realized by the existing ordinary regular centre block.

## Circularity audit

- same-dimensional radicial recursion lowers an inner finite/multiset rank;
- singular supports and flatifying/common-refinement supports have smaller dimension;
- terminal toroidal resolution invokes no general resolution theorem;
- defectless projection is used only after radicial-envelope uniformization;
- Riemann-Zariski compactness is used only after every local word is finite.

## Validation boundary

The public manuscript is internally written as a complete proof. Independent peer review and complete formal verification are not recorded in the archive.
