## 1. The false universal target

A theorem asserting that every finite separable extension of positive-characteristic function fields becomes monomial after birational modifications of regular local models is false. There are defect extensions for which even weak local monomialization fails.

Therefore neither `EVWT` nor any successor theorem may require the full extension of local rings, the complete finite algebra, or every valuation-theoretic function to become monomial.

---

## 2. What the resolution construction actually reads

The resolution state uses a finite family of finitely presented objects:

```text
active marked ideals;
finite normal Hasse jets;
conormal/projectivity/Fitting modules;
exactified Tor--Valabrega diagrams;
finite differential, trace, conductor and ramification shadows;
finite Norm--Rees and descent-gap data;
source, boundary, contact and history filtrations;
comparison maps used to reconstruct the successor state.
```

A finite-state preparation is required to prepare exactly these objects. It does not assert monomiality of the full field extension.

## 3. Conditional finite-state preparation theorem

Assume:

1. the X044 complete-colour actual-centre theorem in lower carrier dimension;
2. the X042 universal exactification theorem;
3. the X043 normal-jet legality and ambient realization theorem;
4. the X049 global Norm--Rees and balanced-gap theorem.

Then every finite event-visible state admits a finite word of ordinary regular jointly legal base blowups such that:

- every coherent rank-one colour is invertible and supported on an SNC divisor;
- every finitely presented module is flat on the relevant rank stratum;
- all defining short exact sequences remain universally exact;
- every active marked containment and finite Hasse-jet condition is monomial;
- every source, boundary and history comparison is preserved;
- every nonzero successor coherent defect has smaller support/Fitting/contact profile.

### Proof architecture

Aggregate the active marked gates exactly, retain all colours separately, and form the complete finite multi-Rees portfolio. Apply simultaneous flattening and enroll the corresponding modification ideals and all overlap defects in the lower-dimensional actual-centre theorem. Resolve the divisorial supports and their intersections relative to the full portfolio. X042--X043 reconstruct every successor chart without reset. X049 prepares the finite wild norm and descent-gap colours base-first.

## 4. Why this does not contradict the counterexamples

The prepared finite state is a finite quotient of the complete valuation-theoretic information. An independent defect extension retains a non-finitely-generated best-generator ideal and a nonattained improvement cut outside every fixed finite coherent packet. The full extension may remain nonmonomial even though every current state observable is monomial or flat.
