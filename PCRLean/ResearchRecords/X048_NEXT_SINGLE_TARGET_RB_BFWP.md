## Ramification-Balanced Base-First Wild Preparation (RB-BFWP)

All further work on the X048 line is frozen to this theorem until it is proved, refuted, or decomposed by a genuine counterexample.

### Exact target

For one independent Artin--Schreier degree-p layer, after finite-flat and toroidal preparation, construct on the original model a finite coherent norm--Rees/Newton shadow portfolio and a finite word of ordinary blowups in regular jointly legal centres such that, uniformly on the certified quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood, every leaf satisfies one of:

1. the normalized pullback monomializes the complete upper event-visible packet and all typed shadows vanish;
2. a nonzero ramification-balanced codimension-at-least-two descent gap, Cech-Rees shadow, or differential-ramification shadow is strictly lowered by the X044 actual-centre block on every chart;
3. effective key degree, conductor height, valuation-support dimension, Frobenius/radicial exponent, typed wild-layer profile, or common-model class strictly drops.

No leaf may create an equal-or-higher class-V token with fresh ancestry.

Do not use alteration as the final resolution, normalization as a base modification, a finite approximation index, a cofinal-tail shift, or the fixed-projection defect factor as a stage rank.
