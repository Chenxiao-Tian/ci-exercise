- a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood;

- one finite event-visible shadow hull;
- a finite regular upper/common model;

1. the finite wild map is diagonal/toroidal in every normal and valuation direction read by the complete state;

5. no equal-or-higher class-V token with new ancestry is created.
