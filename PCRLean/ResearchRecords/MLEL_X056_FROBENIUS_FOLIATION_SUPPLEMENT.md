# X056 Supplement: Height-One Frobenius Sandwiches

A height-one finite radicial morphism `q:Y->X` is encoded by the saturated p-closed logarithmic foliation

```text
F_(Y/X)=Der_X(O_Y) cap T_Y(-log E_Y).
```

The quotient is `O_X=O_Y^F`. A coherent centre ideal stable under the full relative Hasse algebra is the extension of its invariant contraction. The equality holds in every Rees degree, so the normalized quotient blowup agrees with the transformed sandwich.

The intrinsic foliation state is

```text
(nonprojective-support dimension,
 logarithmic differential corank,
 projective-contact multiset,
 Cartier-Hasse profile,
 ordered Fitting/kernel profile,
 aged exceptional history).
```

The complete centre block lowers this state on every nonstraight chart. When all defects vanish, logarithmic Cartier straightening gives etale coordinates in which the foliation is generated by coordinate derivations and logarithmic Euler derivations. Its invariant ring is regular, generated by pth powers in the foliation directions and the untouched transverse coordinates.

For a finite purely inseparable extension `K'/K`, the canonical filtration

```text
K_i=K (K')^(p^(e-i))
```

has height-one layers and removes every arbitrary root-tower choice.
