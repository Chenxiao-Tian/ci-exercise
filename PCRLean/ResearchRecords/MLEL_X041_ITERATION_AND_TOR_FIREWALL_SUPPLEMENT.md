# 1. Purpose

The X041 source-word theorem uses two facts which should not remain bundled
inside one conjectural edge:

1. the cohomology of an iterated Cartier décalage has an exact closed formula;
2. negative Tor cohomology created by a nonflat pullback does not by itself
   contaminate the degree-zero transform at the next step.

The first fact follows by induction from the Berthelot--Ogus cohomology formula.
The second follows from the degree-zero case of that formula and the right
`t`-exactness of derived pullback.  The remaining difficult edge is therefore
not hidden negative Tor.  It is the natural comparison of décalage with a
nonflat common-refinement pullback, for which the good-triple construction is
used.

# 2. Iterated cohomology formula

Let `X` be a ringed space, let `I` be an invertible ideal locally generated by a
nonzerodivisor, and let `K` be an object of `D(O_X)`.  For an `O_X`-module `H`,
write

```text
H[I^N] = {sections locally annihilated by I^N}.
```

The standard one-step formula is

```text
H^i(Leta_I K)
  ~= I^(tensor i) tensor (H^i(K)/H^i(K)[I]).
```

> **Proposition (iterated décalage formula).**  For every `N>=1`, there is a
> natural isomorphism
>
> ```text
> H^i((Leta_I)^N K)
>   ~= I^(tensor N*i) tensor
>       (H^i(K)/H^i(K)[I^N]).
> ```

## Proof

The case `N=1` is the standard formula.  Assume the formula for `N`.  Tensoring
with an invertible module does not change `I`-power torsion.  In the quotient

```text
Q_N=H^i(K)/H^i(K)[I^N],
```

the `I`-torsion submodule is exactly

```text
H^i(K)[I^(N+1)]/H^i(K)[I^N].
```

Indeed the assertion is local, where `I=(f)`: the class of `x` in `Q_N` is
killed by `f` exactly when `f x` is killed by `f^N`, equivalently when `x` is
killed by `f^(N+1)`.  Quotienting `Q_N` by its `I`-torsion therefore gives

```text
H^i(K)/H^i(K)[I^(N+1)].
```

Applying the one-step formula once more adds one copy of `I^(tensor i)` and
proves the induction step.  Naturality follows from naturality of the one-step
formula and of the torsion filtration.  QED.

For `i=0` this becomes

```text
H^0((Leta_I)^N K)
  ~= H^0(K)/H^0(K)[I^N].
```

# 3. Finite stabilization

If the cohomology modules are coherent on a quasi-compact Noetherian scheme,
then for each `i` the ascending chain

```text
H^i(K)[I] subset H^i(K)[I^2] subset ...
```

stabilizes.  A bounded perfect packet has only finitely many nonzero cohomology
sheaves, so one exponent `N` dominates all degrees and every member of a finite
portfolio.  For such `N`, degree-zero cohomology of `(Leta_I)^N` is quotient by
the full `I`-power torsion.

The minimal exponent need not be locally constant and is not used as a
canonical geometric selector.  A capsule may record any functorially supplied
upper bound; larger exponents give the same saturated quotient.

# 4. Degree-zero Tor firewall

Let `f:Y->X` be a morphism and let `K in D^{<=0}(O_X)`.  Derived pullback is
right `t`-exact, and

```text
H^0(Lf^*K) ~= f^*H^0(K).
```

Negative Tor sheaves remain in negative cohomological degrees.  They do not
enter degree zero.  Moreover, the iterated formula shows that

```text
H^0((Leta_I)^N K)
```

depends only on `H^0(K)` and its `I^N`-torsion, even though `Leta` is not an
exact triangulated functor.

Consequently, at the level of one actual module transform, replacing a
connective derived packet by its `H^0` before the next pullback does not lose a
contribution from negative Tor to the next degree-zero saturated transform.
This is the **degree-zero Tor firewall**.

The firewall does not prove factorization independence.  To commute a
décalage already performed on one model with a subsequent nonflat common-
refinement pullback, one still needs either:

```text
flat base change,
or
a good-triple comparison theorem,
or an explicit finite derived defect packet.
```

# 5. One-step strict-transform consequence

On an affine blowup chart with exceptional equation `a`, the ordinary strict
transform of a module `M` is

```text
(M tensor B)/(a-power torsion).
```

Let `K=M tensor^L B`.  Then `H^0(K)=M tensor B`.  If `N` dominates its
`a`-power-torsion exponent, the iterated formula gives

```text
H^0((Leta_a)^N K)
  ~= (M tensor B)/(a-power torsion),
```

which is the strict transform.  Thus the one-chart X041-G4 theorem is reduced
to standard blowup-chart algebra plus the iteration proposition.

The remaining work is sheafification, uniformity over a finite cover, source
labels, exceptional multiplicities, and compatibility under word composition.
