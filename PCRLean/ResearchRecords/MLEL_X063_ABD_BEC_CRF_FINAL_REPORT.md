2. A finite centre-exact word gives a finite labelled cube. Its total fibre reconstructs the state. The cube is unchanged, up to contractible degenerate faces, by inserting the blowup of an invertible ideal, commuting disjoint centres, or passing to hereditary regular common refinements.
3. Essential faces carry the X061 dynamic generic-support cycle. New refinement faces are either source-labelled copies or lie on lower-dimensional carriers or proper supports.
4. A completed actual-centre block lowers the established geometric rank, lowers the generic quotient on an unchanged essential face, or transfers the entire residual event to a lower-dimensional face. The resulting multiset rank is factorization independent.
5. Cartier--Riemann--Hilbert duality matches each leading constructible factor with the nonnilpotent coherent Cartier--Rees quotient whose Fitting support enters the same actual-centre portfolio.
6. Riemann--Zariski compactness, hereditary regular common refinement, and canonical projection-groupoid descent complete the global and functorial theorem.
