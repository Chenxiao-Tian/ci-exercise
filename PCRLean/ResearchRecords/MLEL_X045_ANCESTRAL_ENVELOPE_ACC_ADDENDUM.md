## 1. Why a finite source semilattice is not enough

A finite immutable source universe prevents free root birth and chart cloning, but one source may still support infinitely many different coherent event layers. A finite coupon decomposition is also too presentation dependent. The correct object is one coherent ancestral event envelope and an increasing consumed submodule.

## 2. Fixed finite-type coherent recurrent class

Freeze every coordinate preceding source causality:

```text
ambient and carrier dimension;
Hilbert-Samuel and marked profiles;
Frobenius and direction levels;
projectivity and Fitting profile;
projective contact height;
exactified mixed-Rees profile;
boundary incidence;
common-model class;
presentation ranks, homological amplitudes and operator bounds.
```

Let `R_A` be the finite coloured multi-Rees algebra of an ancestor state and `P_A` the bounded direct sum of all coloured presentation complexes.

The permitted finite grammar consists of kernels, images, cokernels and cones of the exactified diagrams; bounded tensor, symmetric, exterior, divided-power and Hasse constructions in the degrees fixed by the marks; normal-jet truncation; homogeneous localization and centre-exact saturation; finite Fitting layers; and source joins.

## 3. Finite ancestral envelope

> **Finite Ancestral Envelope Theorem.** There exists a bounded coherent complex `U_A` on the ancestor multi-Rees space such that every cohomology Fitting layer of every one-step event that remains in the fixed coherent recurrent class is a subquotient of a hereditary transport of a cohomology sheaf of `U_A`.

The proof applies the finite grammar to finite presentations of `P_A` and takes the finite direct sum of the resulting total complexes. Exactified Tor-Valabrega comparison and ambient trace realization identify successor operations with base change, homogeneous localization and saturation of these ancestor constructions. A transition requiring a new degree, operator level, presentation rank or operation leaves the frozen recurrent class and has already changed an earlier geometric coordinate.

The envelope is Noetherian, not finite length.

## 4. Consumed-source module

Put

```text
U_A^tot = directSum_i H^i(U_A).
```

Along a branch in the fixed class define

```text
0=C_0 subset C_1 subset C_2 subset ... subset U_A^tot.
```

A hereditary event leaves `C_n` unchanged. A merge consumes the inverse images of all parent quotients. A genuinely new primitive Fitting layer is a nonzero quotient of `U_A^tot/C_n`; let `C_(n+1)` be its inverse image.

> **Ancestral No-Repetition Theorem.** Every genuinely new coherent charge gives a strict inclusion `C_n proper subset C_(n+1)`. Hence only finitely many genuinely new charges occur in the fixed coherent recurrent class.

This is Noetherian ACC on submodules. Exceptional history shifts, chart localizations, saturation, source joins and recombinations of consumed traces remain inside the consumed submodule.

## 5. Splitting and cloning

Reducibility of the transform of one coherent source quotient does not split its source budget. All components are restrictions of one global transformed quotient and carry one ancestor quotient ID. Sibling charts are aliases of one descended packet. A decomposition creates separate chargeable packets only when it was already a direct-sum decomposition enrolled in the ancestral envelope.

## 6. Effect on coherent recurrence

After finitely many visits to a fixed coherent SCC, the consumed module stabilizes. Every later event is inherited. The complete X044 block targeted at a nonzero inherited obstruction removes it, merges active sources, lowers a coherent profile, exits the SCC, or completes a finite centre word. A neutral nonidentity cycle would preserve an obstruction after a block constructed to eliminate its complete colour portfolio, contradicting terminal correctness.

Thus the finite-type coherent recurrent subgame terminates, conditionally on the X044 actual-centre theorem and semantic completeness of the coherent colour grammar.

## 7. Sharp frontier

The argument does not apply to an immediate-defect pro-end whose later coefficient and ramification shadows are not contained in one fixed finite grammar. X045 therefore reduces the universal recurrent problem to a projection-free uniform event-visible immediate-defect escape theorem on a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood.

General arbitrary-dimensional positive-characteristic resolution remains open.
