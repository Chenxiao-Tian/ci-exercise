# MLEL-X059 Supplement

## Perverse Postnikov Exactification and Recollement No-Reset

### 1. Proper pushforward is derived

The Cartier-Riemann-Hilbert correspondence commutes with derived proper pushforward, not with an unstated t-exact replacement. The transported residual state must therefore retain all perverse cohomology objects and all connecting maps.

### 2. Bounded amplitude

The initial state is bounded and the operation grammar is finite. On the fixed finite-dimensional initial model there is one perverse amplitude interval `[a_-,a_+]` for all states and events.

### 3. Recollement filtration

For a fixed finite stratification, the maximal perverse subobject supported on each closed union gives a canonical filtration. Its simple factors are intermediate extensions from smooth strata. No splitting is assumed.

### 4. Exact leading quotient

At the highest nonzero perverse degree and maximal support layer, the event triangle yields a long exact sequence. All connecting-map defects are included in the algebraic exactified portfolio. After preparation, either the degree or support layer falls, or the leading map is a strict epimorphism with nonzero kernel.

### 5. Extension classes

The consumed object is an actual subobject in a finite-length category. A nonzero new extension with old simple factors still strictly enlarges the consumed subobject. Thus a continuous family of extensions cannot evade termination by keeping its Jordan-Holder multiset constant.

### 6. No-reset

Chart copies and common-refinement copies have one intermediate-extension identity. Frobenius cleaning and locally nilpotent Cartier changes are zero in the crystal category. A new geometric chamber starts only after an earlier coordinate falls. Hence neither perverse amplitude nor the consumed subobject resets.

### 7. Result

The lexicographic rank `(geometric profile, perverse degree, recollement layer, residual quotient)` is well founded and strictly decreases on every nonterminal completed block.
