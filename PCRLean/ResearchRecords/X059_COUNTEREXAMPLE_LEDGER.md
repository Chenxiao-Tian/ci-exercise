# X059 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. Proper pushforward is not assumed perverse t-exact

A proper birational morphism can produce perverse cohomology in several degrees. The proof retains the full Postnikov tower and its connecting maps.

## 2. Jordan-Holder factors do not determine an object

Two finite-length perverse objects can have the same simple factors and different extension classes. X059 charges actual strict subobjects, not only multisets of factors.

## 3. Recollement filtrations need not split

The graded pieces are intermediate extensions, but the filtration is not replaced by their direct sum. No semisimplicity or decomposition theorem is used.

## 4. Support is not an actual centre

A constructible support may be singular and may fail marked, passive, Tor, or boundary legality. Centres continue to come from the algebraic Fitting portfolio.

## 5. Connecting maps are genuine defects

A defect can move from one perverse degree to another through a connecting morphism. Every kernel, image, and cokernel of these maps is exactified and enrolled before the step is charged.

## 6. Chart restrictions are aliases

Intermediate extension identifies restrictions of one primitive local system to several charts or refinements. They do not create new source identities.

## 7. Valuations do not choose centres

A valuation and defectless projection certify that a residual layer is nonzero and is consumed. The centre is already defined by global algebraic data.

## 8. External validation boundary

Under the user's X059 assumption the public paper is unconditional. The universal Postnikov-recollement termination theorem and its application to general positive-characteristic resolution have not been independently peer reviewed or completely formalized in this archive.
