\begin{abstract}
We propose a simultaneous and functorial legalization mechanism for a finite
arrangement of regular candidate carriers.  Independent flatifier words can
cross other carriers, and an arbitrary choice of one flatifier per carrier is
not made functorial merely by symmetrizing afterwards.  The complete affine
normal-cone module is paired with a finite proper Rees--Serre packet.  Functorial
flatification of the latter gives equivariant component source words.  Each is
compressed to a source-labelled blowup ideal, lifted to an ambient trace ideal,
and the finite source orbit is multiplied.  The blowup of the product is a
common refinement of all flatifiers.  Componentwise admissibility forces the
product support below the maximal carrier dimension, so strong relative
principalization in lower dimension realizes the common modification by
ordinary blowups in regular jointly legal centres.  Source arrangement
completion and deepest-first serialization reduce all remaining interactions
to the disjoint and nested cases.  The latter is governed by a Regular-Flag
Flat-Lift theorem.  The construction is a candidate architecture; its
load-bearing Rees--Serre, scheme, and no-reset theorems remain open.
\end{abstract}

\section{The crossing obstruction}
Let $X=\operatorname{Spec}k[x,y,z]$, $C_1=V(x)$, $C_2=V(y)$, and let
$D=V(x,z)\subset C_1$ be one preparatory flatifier centre.  Then
\[
 D\cap C_2=V(x,y,z),
\]
but $D$ is neither disjoint from nor contained in $C_2$.  Thus independent
carrier preparation followed by concatenation is not closed under ambient
interaction.  The origin must be enrolled as a deeper stratum before $D$ is
executed.

\section{The hidden flatifier choice}
Existence of an admissible flatifier is insufficient for a functorial
resolution algorithm.  Two valid flatifier words may have different source
supports and ambient trace ideals.  Taking a product after selecting one word
does not remove that prior choice.

For a regular carrier $C$, write
\[
 E_C=I_C/I_C^2,\qquad P_C=\mathbf P(E_C),
\]
and let $G_C(M)=\operatorname{gr}_{I_C}(M)$ be the complete affine normal-cone
module of one passive owner.  The affine module is the exact all-degree object,
but general affine flatification does not by itself choose a source word
functorial under smooth and \emph{\'etale} base change.

We therefore retain a finite proper Rees--Serre packet consisting of bounded
irrelevant torsion, finitely many low and transition degrees, the coherent
projective-normal tail on $P_C$, and the comparison and higher-direct-image
certificates.  On the disjoint union of the intrinsic maximal-carrier groupoid,
functorial flatification of this proper packet gives an equivariant admissible
source word.  Its centres need not be regular in positive characteristic; they
are inputs to the lower-dimensional regular realization compiler.

\section{Source ideals and the common product}
Let $\{C_\lambda\}_{\lambda\in\Lambda}$ be the finite set of maximal regular
carriers.  For each $\lambda$, compress the functorial carrier-flatification
word to an immutable source-labelled blowup ideal
\[
 J_\lambda\subset\mathcal O_{C_\lambda}.
\]
Let $K_\lambda\subset\mathcal O_X$ be its inverse-image ambient trace.  Define
\[
 K_{\mathrm{com}}=\prod_{\lambda\in\Lambda}K_\lambda.
\]
The product is independent of the enumeration and invariant under the carrier
groupoid.  The blowup $\operatorname{Bl}_{K_{\mathrm{com}}}X$ factors through
every $\operatorname{Bl}_{K_\lambda}X$, and the strict transform of
$C_\lambda$ in the latter is $\operatorname{Bl}_{J_\lambda}C_\lambda$.

\section{Support-dimension descent}
Assume that each $J_\lambda$ is the unit at every generic point of
$C_\lambda$.  Then
\[
 \dim V(K_\lambda)<\dim C_\lambda.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \dim V(K_{\mathrm{com}})
 < \max_\lambda\dim C_\lambda.
\]
The product centre can be singular and is not an allowed final centre.  Strong
relative principalization below the maximal carrier dimension is applied to
$K_{\mathrm{com}}$, with the complete active, passive, logarithmic, contact,
source, and debt portfolio.  It yields a finite word
\[
 X'\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow X
\]
of ordinary blowups in regular jointly legal centres such that
$K_{\mathrm{com}}\mathcal O_{X'}$ is invertible.  Hence the word factors through
the common blowup and through every source flatifier.

\section{Simultaneous flatness}
Let $F_\lambda^{\mathrm{flat}}$ be a strict transform on the flatified carrier,
flat over $\operatorname{Bl}_{J_\lambda}C_\lambda$.  Its pullback to the final
common refinement is flat.  Additional exceptional equations are Cartier on
the transformed regular carrier and hence act injectively on this flat module.
Thus the source-labelled strict transform along the complete word equals the
pullback of $F_\lambda^{\mathrm{flat}}$.  All maximal carriers are therefore
prepared by one common macro.  The finite Rees--Serre comparison then recovers
flatness of every degree of the affine normal-cone module.

\section{Source arrangement completion}
Adjoin the source supports to the old carrier arrangement and take all
scheme-theoretic intersections.  Nonregular intersections enter the
support--thickness recursion.  When the completion is clean, let $D$ be a
minimal nonempty stratum.  For every stratum $C$,
\[
 D\cap C=\varnothing\quad\text{or}\quad D\subset C.
\]
Indeed, a nonempty intersection is another stratum contained in $D$, so
minimality forces equality.  Hence deepest-first serialization has only a
disjoint case and a nested regular-flag case.

\section{The remaining nested theorem}
The load-bearing geometric statement is the following.

\begin{theorem}[Regular-Flag Flat-Lift, target]
Let $D\subset C\subset X$ be regular immersions in a regular Noetherian scheme,
and let a finite passive owner portfolio be normally flat along $C$.  Suppose
$D$ is jointly legal for the complete Rees and boundary portfolio attached to
$C$.  After blowing up $X$ in $D$, let $C'$ and the owners be strict transforms.
Then, on every standard chart and overlap, the associated graded along $C'$ is
the centre-enriched strict transform of the old associated graded, with the
canonical exceptional grading twist.  In particular it is flat over $C'$.
The comparison commutes with smooth base change and preserves all source,
owner, boundary, contact, and debt identities.
\end{theorem}

This theorem is not proved here.  Together with Hironaka normal-flatness
transitivity it implies passive persistence through the complete wonderful
word.

\section{Candidate functorial common-trace theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Functorial Common-Trace Carrier Legalization, target]
Let a prepared marked state on a smooth scheme over a perfect field of positive
characteristic produce a finite symmetry-stable clean arrangement of regular
raw carriers.  Assume strong relative principalization and the same
legalization program in lower carrier dimension.  The intrinsic groupoid of
maximal carriers admits a finite proper Rees--Serre packet whose functorial
flatification gives equivariant component source words.  Their ambient trace
ideals admit one common product whose support has lower dimension.  A finite
word of ordinary blowups in regular jointly legal centres realizes the common
refinement and factors through every source flatifier.  All maximal carriers
become normally flat simultaneously, and the proper and affine flatness
certificates agree.  After source arrangement completion, every wonderful
interaction is disjoint or nested, and the Regular-Flag Flat-Lift theorem
preserves passive legality and all hereditary data.  No passive charge is
created by flatification, completion, or serialization.
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}
The theorem is a maximum-likelihood target.  The finite functorial Rees--Serre
packet, lower-dimensional full-portfolio principalization,
source-pure-transform comparison, Regular-Flag Flat-Lift, and all-chart no-reset
remain open.  General resolution in positive characteristic is not asserted.
\end{remark}
