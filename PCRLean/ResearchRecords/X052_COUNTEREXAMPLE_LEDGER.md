# X052 Counterexample and Failure-Boundary Ledger

## 1. Birational models do not change valued-field defect

For a fixed finite extension `F|E` and fixed extension of valuations, changing regular or singular models does not change `e`, `f`, the henselian local degree, or the defect. Any proposed blowup rank which claims otherwise confuses a field invariant with a model invariant.

## 2. Best generators are field-theoretic

Existence of a best Artin--Schreier generator is a property of the fixed valued-field extension. Ordinary blowups can expose new coherent approximants but cannot create a best generator after all finite-state shadows have stabilized.

## 3. Independent/dependent type is invariant

The independent/dependent classification is determined by the Artin--Schreier distance. A model change cannot turn an independent defect extension into a dependent one.

## 4. Coarsening is classification, not progress

Passing from `v` to `v/Delta` may normalize an idempotent distance to `0^-`, but it changes the valuation used to describe the same field extension and does not perform a blowup.

## 5. A projection switch can lower only to the minimum

The defect of the current projection is not intrinsic, but the minimum over all admissible projections is. A finite list or an enumeration of projections cannot be a well-founded progress measure after a minimizing projection is reached.

## 6. Finite-state preparation does not eliminate a projection-minimal token

Marked ideals, Hasse coefficients, modules, and Rees shadows may all become monomial or flat while the finite henselian extension attached to the projection remains defective. The field token must be handled by a different projection.

## 7. Alteration uniformization does not prove DPE on the original field

Local uniformization after a finite extension or purely inseparable alteration supplies a regular upper model, but the rational subfield and valued-field extension have changed. Descent back to the original function field is exactly the unresolved issue.

## 8. Generic projection is not known to be defectless

Generic separability over a perfect field guarantees a finite separable projection, not a defectless one for the chosen valuation.

## 9. Local uniformization gives a defectless projection

At a regular smooth centre, etale coordinates yield a finite etale local branch and therefore defect zero. Thus any genuine counterexample to DPE would also obstruct local uniformization.

## 10. Truth boundary

DPE is known in Abhyankar and specified tame-base chambers. It is open in general. The general resolution theorem remains conditional on DPE and on the previously isolated coherent interfaces.
