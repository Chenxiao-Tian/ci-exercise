# X062 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. Fitting ideals of arbitrary Cartier representatives are not intrinsic

Nil-isomorphic representatives can differ by nilpotent submodules or quotients and therefore by presentation matrices and Fitting ideals. X062 uses the unique minimal representative before forming any centre carrier.

## 2. Vanishing in the crystal category is not actual module vanishing

An arbitrary representative may be nonzero but nilpotent. Flat-kill cannot be applied to that statement. For a minimal representative, a nil-isomorphism to zero is an actual isomorphism to zero.

## 3. A perverse support is not a scheme-theoretic centre

The constructible leading factor identifies the event. The actual centre comes from the Fitting tower of the Cartier-dual minimal quotient together with the full legality portfolio.

## 4. Flatification centres need not be regular

Raynaud--Gruson flattening supplies a modification, not automatically an allowed regular centre. The modification ideal and all legality data are realized by the existing actual-centre theorem.

## 5. Proper support reduction is not automatic

A proper subset of a current carrier may still dominate the old support. X062 uses relative flatness and generic vanishing to prove that no successor quotient component dominates the eliminated support.

## 6. Strict transform must retain scheme-theoretic images

Replacing strict transform by set-theoretic closure can create false exceptional components. X062 uses the quotient by exceptional-power torsion and the scheme-theoretic-image compatibility of strict transforms.

## 7. Validation boundary

The public article is unconditional under the user's X062 assumption. The new minimal-carrier matching, Fitting-exact generic annihilation, and support-causal descent theorems have not been independently peer reviewed or fully formalized in this archive.
