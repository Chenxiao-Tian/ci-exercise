# X062 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. Proper direct image is not conservative

For a codimension-two regular blowup, the nonzero exceptional sheaf `O_E(-1)` has zero derived pushforward. Root transport cannot be the sole event carrier.

## 2. One exceptional restriction is not enough for gluing

The data `j^*K` and `i^*K` identify the pieces but do not by themselves retain every attachment map. X062 keeps both `i^*`, `i^!`, and the two recollement morphisms.

## 3. An exceptional event is not a free new source

Every exceptional node inherits the source join of the centre which created it. Chart and common-refinement copies are aliases.

## 4. Constructible exceptional support is not automatically an ambient centre

The event is dualized to a coherent Cartier-Rees quotient. Its Fitting support enters the complete joint-legality portfolio and is ambientized by the regular-flag theorem.

## 5. Dimension alone is insufficient inside one exceptional carrier

X062 uses the X061 perverse degree, actual support inclusion, and generic length after carrier dimension.

## 6. Normalization and exceptional probes are not final modifications

The output remains a finite sequence of ordinary blowups in regular centres. Recollement and normalization are comparison devices.

## 7. External validation boundary

The public article is unconditional under the user's X062 assumption. The full recursive strict alternative and final general theorem have not been independently peer reviewed or completely formalized in this archive.
