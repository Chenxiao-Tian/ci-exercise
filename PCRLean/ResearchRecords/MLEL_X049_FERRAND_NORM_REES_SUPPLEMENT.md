## 1. Definition without generators

For a finite locally free morphism `f:Y->X` of rank `n`, Ferrand's norm functor gives a functor on quasi-coherent modules and algebras, compatible with arbitrary base change. For a coherent ideal `J->O_Y`, define

```text
N_f(J)=image(Norm_f(J)->Norm_f(O_Y)=O_X).
```

The definition is intrinsic. It does not choose generators, a Galois closure, a trace basis, or a splitting of the finite algebra.

For a nonflat later modification, retain the map `Norm_f(J)->O_X`, its image, kernel and cokernel. The norm functor commutes with base change; exactified image formation is treated as in X042.

---

## 2. Elementary properties

- `N_f(J)` is coherent on a Noetherian base.
- Flat base change commutes with `N_f(J)`.
- An isomorphism of finite covers carrying `J` to `J'` carries the norm ideal to the norm ideal.
- `N_f(J)N_f(K) subset N_f(JK)`.
- If `J=O_Y`, then `N_f(J)=O_X`.
- If `J` is locally generated by one section `a`, the norm ideal is generated by the determinant norm of `a`.

The last statement explains the connection with classical ideal norms but the construction remains valid for noninvertible ideals.

## 3. Diagonal toroidal computation

Let

```text
x_i=u_i^(e_i),
n=product e_i,
J=(u^(alpha^(1)),...,u^(alpha^(m))).
```

The norm of a monomial is a unit times

```text
x_1^((n/e_1)alpha_1)...x_r^((n/e_r)alpha_r).
```

The norms of the chosen monomial generators give the vertices of the scaled Newton polyhedron. Every term in the norm of a general element of `J` lies in the same polyhedron. Therefore

```text
overline(N_f(J))
 = overline((x^((n/e)alpha^(a)))_a),

overline(N_f(J)O_Y)=overline(J^n).
```

The statement remains valid in inseparable diagonal charts: the norm is then a power polynomial law, and integral closure removes the absence of mixed polarization terms.

## 4. Norm--Rees shadow

On an excellent base define

```text
NR_f(J)=normalization(Rees_X(N_f(J))).
```

This is a finite graded algebra. Its relative Proj is a canonical normalized modification. In a toroidal chart it is the normal fan modification of the upper Newton polyhedron.

The normalized modification is only a comparison object. The final resolution uses an ordinary regular centre word dominating it. In a monomial chamber this is obtained by regular fan subdivisions; in the general candidate architecture the modification ideal and all semantic colours enter the X044 actual-centre theorem.

---

## 5. Why colours are not replaced by one norm ideal

For a split cover `Y=X disjoint-union X` and an ideal pair `(J_1,J_2)`, the norm shadow is `J_1J_2`. It forgets the ordered branch labels and the owner/source semantics. Those data remain separate colours. The norm ideal supplies the common base modification, not the complete successor state.

Likewise, a norm ideal can be nontrivial even when one branch is already terminal. The all-chart state still decides which transformed branch remains active.

---

## 6. Boundary

Outside the toroidal or otherwise controlled chamber, the norm ideal remains well defined but the identity with the upper Newton modification is not proved. This is precisely the Event-Visible Wild Toroidalization frontier.
