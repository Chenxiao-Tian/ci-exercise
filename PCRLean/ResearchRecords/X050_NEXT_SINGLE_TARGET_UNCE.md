# X050 Single-Target Focus Freeze

## Uniform Nonattained-Cut Escape (UNCE)

All further work on the X050 line is frozen to this theorem until it is proved, refuted, or decomposed by a genuine counterexample.

### Exact target

Let one independent Artin--Schreier degree-p defect layer be represented by:

- a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood;
- a finite prepared event-visible state on one common model;
- immutable valued-field, projection, source and wild-layer ancestry;
- the full non-finitely-generated best-generator ideal in the valuation ring;
- its stable nonattained improvement cut;
- all finite coherent contractions, Hasse, differential, trace, conductor, Norm--Rees and Cech-Rees shadows.

Construct a finite portfolio of ordinary regular jointly legal base words and finite reprojections such that every valuation follows one branch and every nonterminal leaf satisfies one of:

1. a best generator exists and the layer is defectless;
2. the layer is dependent and enters the radicial/Frobenius branch;
3. the cut or its pro-isomorphism/isolated-subgroup class strictly changes;
4. effective key degree, conductor height, valuation-support dimension or common-model class strictly drops;
5. a new nonzero coherent contraction appears and re-enters X049 finite-state preparation.

The construction must be uniform on the whole certified neighbourhood and must create no equal-or-higher class-V token.

### Minimal proof cut

```text
UNCE-1  intrinsic finite presentation of the reprojection portfolio;
UNCE-2  compatibility of best-generator ideals and cuts under reprojection;
UNCE-3  effectivity of the first newly visible coherent contraction;
UNCE-4  strict isolated-subgroup/cut change in the shadow-free branch;
UNCE-5  all-chart source and valuation ancestry transport;
UNCE-6  common-refinement and smooth/etale compatibility;
UNCE-7  no equal-or-higher independent-defect birth.
```

### Forbidden diversions

Until UNCE is resolved, do not introduce:

- another general invariant;
- another source ledger;
- another centre grammar;
- another recurrent-class taxonomy;
- another manuscript architecture;
- full local monomialization as a universal target;
- a finite list of Artin--Schreier generators as the full defect carrier;
- a cofinal-tail index or number of improvements as rank;
- the defect factor of a fixed projection as a stage counter.

### Immediate work order

1. Work with one independent Artin--Schreier degree-p valuation and its best-generator ideal.
2. Describe the change of the improvement cut under one finite linear reprojection.
3. Prove or falsify the trichotomy: best f, new coherent contraction, or strict isolated-subgroup change.
4. Extend to a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood.
5. Only then compile finite reprojection portfolios and the class-V ordinal.

The X050 line remains `OPEN_GAP`; general positive-characteristic resolution is not established.
