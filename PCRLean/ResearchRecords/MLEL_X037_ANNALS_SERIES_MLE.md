# Projected series abstract

We construct a functorial embedded resolution algorithm for reduced schemes of
finite type over a perfect field of positive characteristic.  A marked
singularity is encoded by a finite intrinsic Frobenius--Hasse state carrying
active and passive owners, an ordered boundary, and fixed-ancestry source and
debt data.  Affine conormal equations define a cokernel obstruction whose
content and projectivity discriminants yield graph, hybrid, unit,
Schur--Fitting, and radicial chambers.

Nonunique permissible closures are retained as a finite intersection

lower-dimensional relative resolution.  Regular-support intersections are
encoded by a finite anchor-contact portfolio.

For every raw centre, all passive and contact data are compressed into finitely
many low associated-graded pieces and coherent sheaves on the projectivized

comparison shows that the actual ambient packet has the same exceptional

without reset.  A composite well-founded rank eliminates exceptional
saturation, contact, births, recharge, kangaroo recurrence, singular Fitting
kernels, and immediate defect.  Descent and symmetry-compatible serialization
produce a finite global sequence of ordinary blowups, yielding
principalization, embedded resolution, and nonembedded resolution.
