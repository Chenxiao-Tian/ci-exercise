# X057 Counterexample and Boundary Ledger

## 1. Locally free p-foliation is not automatically coordinate

Local freeness and p-closure alone do not produce additive or logarithmic coordinate derivations. Bracket, restricted-power, primitive, invariant-ring, boundary, and descent obstructions must vanish. X057 records them in the Cartier--Spencer object; the straightening theorem is invoked only at its zero value.

## 2. Root equations are not intrinsic

Different radicial generators and different degree-p towers can present the same finite purely inseparable extension. A rank or centre rule attached to one chosen equation can change under a new frame. X057 works with the intrinsic Morita algebra, the foliation, the Cartier--Spencer object, and the canonical Frobenius filtration.

## 3. A root lattice does not determine the normalization

The generic group extension records neither the positive cone nor its faces, conductor, or saturation holes. The terminal object must be the fine saturated Kummer monoid and the finite hole module `O[Q^sat]/O[Q]`.

## 4. Normalization is not an allowed final modification

The normalization of a Kummer chart may be regular after a fan subdivision, but normalization itself is not a sequence of ordinary blowups on the original smooth ambient scheme. X057 uses it only as a comparison object and realizes the subdivision downstairs through Ferrand norm ideals and ordinary regular centres.

## 5. One fan need not be regular for two lattices without proof

A simultaneous-two-lattice regular fan statement cannot be used as an unexplained terminal black box. X057 replaces it by source star subdivisions and base-first Norm--Rees principalization with an explicit multiplicity multiset.

## 6. A finite list of coherent modules does not itself give termination

The same support can reappear under new presentations, source merges, and Frobenius transitions. X057 passes to the finite-length category of Cartier crystals and uses Jordan--Holder factors modulo locally nilpotent noise.

## 7. Crystal length alone is not enough

A centre may replace one simple factor by several factors on smaller supports. The order must be the Dershowitz--Manna extension of labels including support dimension, generic rank, owner, source, and boundary stratum, and it must be preceded by the geometric coordinates.

## 8. A valuation projection must not choose a centre

Defectless projections depend on a valuation and are generally noncanonical. If they enter the centre rule, smooth functoriality is lost. X057 uses them only to form quotient crystals certifying strict descent of the intrinsic state.

## 9. Nilpotent Cartier summands are presentation noise

Kernels and cokernels created by a frame change or an exactified resolution can be nonzero as modules while disappearing after sufficiently high Frobenius iteration. They must be killed in the Cartier-crystal category rather than counted as new geometric obstructions.

## 10. The public/archival distinction

The public manuscript is written as a complete proof under the explicit X057 assumption. This archive does not record independent peer review, external certification, or complete formal verification of the novel Cartier--Spencer--Kummer and Cartier-crystal closure theorems.
