# X056 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. Repeated unit absorption is not automatic

In `R=k[x,x^{-1}]`, the unit `u=x^p` satisfies `du=0`, but its pth root `x` has nonzero differential. Hence `du=0` does not justify an unrecorded repetition until `u` becomes a `p^e`th power.

## 2. Root lattice without monoid saturation is incomplete

A finite-index lattice records the generic torus but not the positive cone, its faces, or normalization. Terminalization must use the saturated Kummer monoid and its cone complex.

## 3. Unit twists are not discarded

A non-p-divisible unit may increase logarithmic Jacobian rank. It is retained until the row-reduction theorem splits the regular block. It is not silently replaced by 1.

## 4. Tower order is not data

Different degree-p factorizations of one finite purely inseparable extension must yield the same saturated operator state and Kummer monoid. Any rank depending on tower length or order is rejected.

## 5. Validation boundary

The public X056 article is written as the assumed final proof. Independent peer review and complete formal certification are not recorded in the archive.
