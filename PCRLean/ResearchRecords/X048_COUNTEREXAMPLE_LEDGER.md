# X048 Counterexample and Failure-Boundary Ledger

## 1. A regular upper Cartier centre need not descend

Let `A=k[t]`, `B=k[s]`, `t=s^e`, and `J=(s)`. The centre `V(s)` is regular upstairs. Its lower and upper descent ideals are `(t)` and `A`; hence it does not descend. The only downstairs divisor with the same support is `V(t)`, whose blowup is the identity.

## 2. Nonzero Cech discrepancy need not be lower-dimensional

In the same example the descent discrepancy is supported at the generic point of the ramification divisor. It is not a codimension-two defect and cannot be removed by a base blowup. Thus the X047 phrase “every nonzero discrepancy layer is prepared downstairs” requires the ramification-residue firewall.

## 3. Group invariance remains insufficient

The ideal `(s)` can be invariant up to unit under the visible automorphism group and still have nonzero divisorial residue. Full Cech descent detects the failure, but no purification can make the same centre descend.

## 4. Pullback of a regular base centre can be nonreduced

Under `x=u^e`, the pullback of the regular base divisor `(x)` is `(u^e)`, not the reduced regular upper centre `(u)`. In higher codimension, the pullback of `(x_1,...,x_r)` is `(u_1^(e_1),...,u_r^(e_r))`, generally nonregular. Exact stagewise matching of regular centres on both sides is therefore not a universal requirement.

## 5. Product or orbit ideals do not automatically give common ordinary blowups

Invertibility of a product ideal does not by itself imply invertibility of each factor. A product/norm modification must be analysed through its Rees algebra or Newton fan, not declared a common refinement without proof.

## 6. Norm--Newton is a normalized comparison

The equality is between integral closures and normalized blowups. The ordinary pullback of the base blowup can be nonnormal. Normalization is diagnostic upstairs and cannot be substituted for the final base resolution.

## 7. One ramification ideal is not a complete wild detector

Norm--Newton preparation addresses monomial active data. Differential, trace, conductor, Tor--Valabrega, source, and Cech discrepancies remain separate colours. A monomial norm ideal cannot erase the independent-defect portfolio.

## 8. Local diagonalization is an additional hypothesis

The exact Newton calculation uses a diagonal toroidal chart. A general finite wild morphism must first be toroidalized for the complete event-visible packet, and the local norm--Newton ideals must glue. This is an open component of RB-BFWP.

## 9. Divisorial balance does not imply full descent

Vanishing codimension-one residues means only that the remaining descent gap has no divisorial support. Codimension-two Cech, extension, and Rees discrepancies can remain and must be prepared as coherent colours.

## 10. Descent of the normalized modification is not descent of an upper centre word

The base-first theorem seeks a base modification whose normalized pullback principalizes the upper packet. It deliberately does not recover the chosen upper regular centres or their ordering.

## 11. Truth boundary

The finite-flat descent interval, ramification firewall, and diagonal norm--Newton theorem are closed. Global wild toroidalization, gluing of the norm--Rees shadow, strict reduction of balanced residual shadows, RB-BFWP, UDE-EV, global termination, and general positive-characteristic resolution remain open.
