# MLEL-X063 Supplement

## Abstract blowup reconstruction and factorization-independent event cubes

A regular blowup is represented by the complete square formed by the source, target, centre, and exceptional divisor. The state retains the open and closed restrictions, both recollement triangles, all gluing maps, and the algebraic Cartier--Rees decorations. The abstract-blowup triangle reconstructs the torsion etale complex; universal exactification reconstructs ideals, Rees algebras, kernels, images, cokernels, multiplication maps, and source/boundary filtrations.

Iterating the square over a finite centre-exact word produces a finite source-labelled cube. Its total fibre is conservative. The blowup of an invertible ideal contributes an identity face. Disjoint blowups form the same Cartesian square in either order. A hereditary common refinement creates only contractible copies or proper exceptional faces. Immutable source labels identify copies before totalization.

Every essential face carries the dynamic generic-support cycle. A completed actual-centre block either lowers a pre-existing geometric coordinate, gives a strict epimorphic quotient with nonzero generic kernel, or moves the event to a proper face of lower carrier dimension. Hence the finite multiset of face tokens decreases independently of the chosen factorization.
