# X053 Single-Target Focus Freeze

## Pointwise Defectless Projection Existence (PDPE)

All further valuation work is frozen to this theorem until it is proved, refuted, or decomposed by a genuine counterexample.

### Exact target

Let `(F|k,v)` be a valued function field over a perfect field, of transcendence degree `d`. Prove that there is a separating transcendence basis

```text
t=(t_1,...,t_d)
```

such that `F/k(t)` is finite separable and the selected henselian branch is defectless:

```text
[F^h:k(t)^h]=e_t f_t.
```

Equivalently, prove

```text
epsilon_pr(F,v/k)=0.
```

### What X053 supplies after PDPE

No additional finite-cover valuation theorem is required for existence. The defectless projection gives a finite local word; its terminal basin is open; Riemann-Zariski quasi-compactness gives finitely many words; hereditary regular common refinement compiles them into one finite global resolution.

### Minimal proof cut

```text
PDPE-1 reduction by a standard valuation transcendence basis;
PDPE-2 elimination of immediate transcendence-defect layers of arbitrary rank;
PDPE-3 compatibility with finite tame-wild towers;
PDPE-4 construction of one defectless separating rational subfield;
PDPE-5 stability under finite common-model refinement;
PDPE-6 verification on the known Abhyankar and tame trdeg-one chambers;
PDPE-7 counterexample audit against independent Artin-Schreier defect.
```

### Forbidden diversions

Until PDPE is resolved, do not introduce:

- a model-stage counter for a fixed projection;
- coarsening as geometric progress;
- another source ledger or recurrent taxonomy;
- a stronger uniform valuation hypothesis for mere existence;
- a full monomialization target contradicted by defect examples;
- alteration as the final resolution;
- another manuscript architecture.

### Separate functorial question

Smooth-functorial resolution additionally requires Functorial Projection-Atlas Selection. Do not mix that canonicalization question into the pointwise valued-field theorem.

### Truth boundary

Pointwise DPE is open in general. General arbitrary-dimensional positive-characteristic resolution is not established.
