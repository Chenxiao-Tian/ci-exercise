# MLEL-X047 Supplement

## Noetherian Purification of a Finite Amitsur Discrepancy

**Parent:** MLEL-X047 / WAD-GCD-UWP-FINAL  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** conditional finite-iteration theorem; Uniform Wild Preparation remains open.

---

## 1. Setup

Let `f:Y->X` be finite faithfully flat, with `X` reduced and Noetherian. Let `Q_Y` be a finite complete coloured object, and assume a finite upper ordinary centre word `w_Y` has been chosen. Let

```text
D(Q_Y,w_Y)
```

be the complete Cech--Rees discrepancy on the two- and three-fold fibre products, pushed to `X`.

The complete discrepancy contains every map needed to reconstruct the word and its successor state. A smaller discrepancy which remembers only modules, only ideals, or only the double-overlap comparison is not sufficient.

---

## 2. Purification step

Assume `D` is nonzero. Choose the first nonzero primitive cohomology/Fitting layer `L` in the fixed order. The support of `L` avoids the good open. Add `L` and all its legality data to the complete downstairs portfolio and apply the X044 source-closed actual-centre block.

Pull the block to `Y`, reconstruct the upper state by X042--X043, and form the new canonical upper word and discrepancy `D'`.

The exactified comparison yields a morphism

```text
Her(D) -> D'
```

whose cone is a one-step X045 event. Both terms and the cone lie in the fixed finite ancestor envelope while all earlier profiles remain constant.

---

## 3. The consumed discrepancy module

Let `U` be the direct sum of the cohomology modules of the finite ancestor envelope. A primitive discrepancy layer is represented by a subquotient of `U`. Define `C_n subset U` to be the submodule generated by all layers already charged before round `n`.

- hereditary transport does not change `C_n`;
- chart restriction creates no new generator;
- a source join uses the same finite support label;
- a recombination of consumed layers remains in `C_n`;
- a genuinely new primitive layer has nonzero image in `U/C_n`.

Thus a genuinely new layer gives a strict inclusion

```text
C_n proper subset C_(n+1).
```

Noetherianity of `U` excludes infinitely many such rounds.

---

## 4. Earlier-profile escape

The fixed ancestor envelope is used only while the following data are constant:

```text
carrier and support dimension;
marked, Frobenius and radicial level;
projective contact height;
Tor-Valabrega-Fitting profile;
wild-layer and common-model class;
upper monomial face profile;
presentation and operator bounds.
```

If any datum changes, the process leaves the chamber and lowers the earlier lexicographic coordinate. It is not counted as a new discrepancy round in the same envelope.

---

## 5. Termination theorem

Order states by

```text
(earlier geometric profile,
 foundation rank of C_n in U).
```

At every nonzero-discrepancy round, the first coordinate decreases or `C_n` strictly increases, which strictly lowers its foundation rank. The order is well founded. Therefore the iteration reaches a state with zero complete discrepancy.

Apply finite-flat descent to obtain the downstairs centre word.

---

## 6. Boundary of the argument

The theorem does not construct the first upper word and does not prove that a finite uniformizing alteration simultaneously monomializes the complete coloured state. That is Uniform Wild Preparation. It also depends on the X044 actual-centre theorem and the X045 complete coherent grammar. These hypotheses remain explicit.
