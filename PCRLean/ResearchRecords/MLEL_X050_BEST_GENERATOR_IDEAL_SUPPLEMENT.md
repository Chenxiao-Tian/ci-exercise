# MLEL-X050 Supplement

## Best Artin--Schreier Generators and the Non-Finitely-Generated Ramification Ideal

**Parent:** MLEL-X050 / BGI-FSP-NCE-FINAL  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** exact valuation-theoretic theorem.

---

## 1. Setup

Let `A` be a henselian valuation ring of equal characteristic `p>0`, with fraction field `K`. Let `L/K` be a nontrivial Artin--Schreier extension of degree `p`.

Let

```text
AS(L/K)={f in K^* | T^p-T-f defines L/K}.
```

Define

```text
H(L/K)=(1/f | f in AS(L/K)) subset A.
```

This is the ramification ideal denoted `H` in the ramification theory of Artin--Schreier extensions.

---

## 2. Best generators

A representative `f` is best when its valuation is maximal among all Artin--Schreier right-hand sides defining the same extension. Equivalently, no translation `f+h^p-h` has strictly larger valuation.

### Theorem 2.1

The following are equivalent:

1. `H(L/K)` is finitely generated;
2. `H(L/K)` is principal;
3. a best Artin--Schreier generator exists;
4. `L/K` is defectless.

### Proof

A finitely generated ideal of a valuation ring is generated by an element of minimum valuation among a finite generating set. Hence (1) implies (2).

Assume `H=(1/f_1,...,1/f_m)`. Choose `1/f_i` of least valuation. Then `v(f_i)` is maximal among `v(f_j)`. If a better representative `g` existed, then `v(1/g)<v(1/f_i)`, so `1/g` would not belong to the principal ideal generated by `1/f_i`, contradicting the definition of `H`. Thus `f_i` is best and (2) implies (3).

If `f_best` is best, then for every representative `f` one has `v(f)<=v(f_best)`, hence `v(1/f)>=v(1/f_best)`. Since ideals in a valuation ring are linearly ordered, `1/f` belongs to `(1/f_best)`. Therefore `H=(1/f_best)` and (3) implies (2).

The equivalence of the existence of best `f` and defectlessness is the degree-`p` Artin--Schreier best-generator theorem for henselian valuation rings.

---

## 3. Consequences

### Corollary 3.1

Every genuine Artin--Schreier defect extension has a non-finitely-generated ramification ideal `H(L/K)`.

### Corollary 3.2

No coherent ideal on a finite Noetherian model can pull back exactly to `H(L/K)` for a defect extension.

### Proof

A coherent ideal on a Noetherian model is finitely generated. Its extension to a valuation ring is a finitely generated, hence principal, ideal. Apply Theorem 2.1.

### Corollary 3.3

The class-V carrier is not a missing coherent centre. It is a genuine noncoherent valuation ideal, or equivalently a nonattained improvement cut.

---

## 4. Finite approximants

For a finite subset `F subset AS(L/K)`, put

```text
H_F=(1/f | f in F).
```

Then `H_F` is principal. If `g` is better than every element of `F`, then

```text
H_F proper subset H_(F union {g}).
```

After contraction to a fixed Noetherian ancestor model, such strict enlargements can occur only finitely often. If improvements continue after stabilization, they are invisible to the fixed coherent state.

---

## 5. Scope

This theorem does not construct an escape from a nonattained cut. It proves that the cut cannot be replaced by one coherent ideal or by a finite list of Artin--Schreier generators. The remaining geometric problem is Uniform Nonattained-Cut Escape.
