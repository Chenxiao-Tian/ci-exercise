# X060 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. Perverse cohomology objects do not determine the derived object

Two bounded complexes may have identical perverse cohomology and different Postnikov extension classes. X060 retains the full Postnikov tower, all connecting morphisms, and residual finite-dimensional Ext coordinates.

## 2. A finite stratification does not imply finite monodromy

One stratum may carry local systems factoring through infinitely many finite quotients. X060 requires a common finite monodromy hull and uniform rank bounds before finite length is used.

## 3. A total order on incomparable supports is not functorial

Smooth pullback or symmetry can permute incomparable supports. Canonical Serre quotients and the maximal active support antichain replace arbitrary ordering.

## 4. Jordan--Holder multiplicities alone lose extension data

Nonsplit extensions can have the same simple constituents. Actual consumed subobjects and Ext-coordinate subspaces, rather than multiplicities alone, enter the rank.

## 5. Cartier--Riemann--Hilbert is contravariant

A leading perverse kernel corresponds to a Cartier quotient. Reversing this orientation can attach the centre to the wrong coherent object.

## 6. Raw smooth pullback is not perverse t-exact

For a smooth map of relative dimension d, all perverse-degree statements use the normalized pullback `g^*[d]`.

## 7. Constructible supports do not define scheme centres

Centres are selected from coherent Cartier--Rees quotients, Fitting ideals, Hasse data, passive exactification, and boundary legality. The constructible envelope supplies identity and termination only.
