# X063 Counterexample and Firewall Ledger

## 1. A literal recollement tree depends on factorization

The same centre-exact modification may be written using different orders of disjoint centres or compared through different regular refinements. Counting literal tree nodes would assign different ranks to the same geometric state. X063 counts only essential nondegenerate faces in the complete blowup hypercube.

## 2. Inserting an identity blowup must not create progress

The blowup of an invertible ideal is an isomorphism. Any rank that charges its formal exceptional face can be lowered by inserting vacuous steps and is not intrinsic. Such faces are contractible and degenerate.

## 3. Common-refinement copies are not births

Pulling a source event to a common refinement may create several chart manifestations. Source labels and hypercube gluing identify them before the multiset is formed.

## 4. Proper pushforward is not conservative

A nonzero exceptional object may have zero root direct image. The full square and open--exceptional recollement are retained; root transport alone is never used as a conservative detector.

## 5. Abstract blowup-square descent is not unrestricted cdh descent

The proof uses precise finite gluing statements for the torsion etale complexes and finitely presented decorations occurring in the state. It does not assume descent for arbitrary objects or arbitrary topologies.

## 6. Factorization invariance does not permit arbitrary singular modifications

Only ordinary blowups in coherent regular jointly legal centres occur in the delivered sequence. Normalizations and singular common modifications are comparison devices and must be realized by the actual-centre theorem.

## 7. Literature boundary

The standard abstract blowup-square, recollement, norm, Cartier-crystal, and multiset-order results do not by themselves prove X063's complete labelled hyperdescent or general positive-characteristic resolution.
