# X063 Counterexample and firewall ledger

1. **Factorization length is not a rank.** The blowup of an invertible ideal is the identity but increases the length of a word.
2. **Tree depth is not intrinsic.** Two disjoint regular centres can be blown up in either order, producing different literal trees and the same Cartesian modification.
3. **Common refinements duplicate strata.** Without immutable source labels, pulling two local words to a common refinement charges the same event more than once.
4. **Proper pushforward is not conservative.** A nonzero exceptional object can have zero direct image; X063 retains the full cube, not only its root transport.
5. **cdh context is not a blanket descent theorem.** The proof invokes only the precise abstract-blowup reconstruction for the finite labelled cubes and separately descends every Cartier--Rees decoration.
6. **A constructible face is not a centre.** Actual centres are defined by coherent Cartier--Rees quotients and their complete active/passive/boundary portfolio.
7. **Normalization is not the delivered modification.** Kummer normalization and normalized Rees spaces are comparison devices; the output remains ordinary blowups in regular centres.
8. **A refinement may add faces without adding geometry.** Degenerate identity faces and source-labelled aliases are removed before the event multiset is charged.
