## 1. Why class V remains categorically different

Every X044 successor event complex has coherent cohomology on a finite model. An independent immediate-defect ramification cut need not be the pullback of any coherent ideal on a finite Noetherian model. Hence it cannot be silently converted into another coherent event colour.

A class-V token consists of:

```text
an immutable valued-field/projection ancestry ID;
a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood;
a finite common model;
finite event-visible coefficient and ramification shadows;
the pro-isomorphism class of the unresolved approximation end.
```

The exact defect factor of a fixed valued-field extension is unchanged by replacing the models by birational blowups. It is not a stage rank.

## 2. Event-visible shadow hull

Although the full pro-end is noncoherent, the current finite coloured state observes only finitely many coefficient ideals, key degrees, Hasse packets, ramification shadows, and source labels. On a quasi-compact valuation neighbourhood these finitely many observables admit one finite common model.

The event-visible shadow hull is the finite tuple

```text
(common model class,
 effective key degree,
 Frobenius/radicial exponent,
 contracted coefficient ideals,
 ramification/conductor shadows,
 valuation support dimension,
 source ancestry).
```

A valid action on a class-V token must be uniform on the whole neighbourhood, not on one chosen approximant.

## 3. Uniform event-visible defect escape UDE-EV

The remaining theorem is:

> For every class-V packet there exists one finite word of actual regular jointly legal centres on a finite sequence of common models such that every valuation in the certified neighbourhood follows the word and every nonterminal leaf satisfies at least one of:
>
> 1. the packet becomes coherent/algebraizable and enters the X045 coherent subgame;
> 2. effective key degree, Frobenius/radicial exponent, valuation support dimension, or conductor height strictly drops;
> 3. a fixed ancestor coefficient ideal strictly enlarges in a Noetherian carrier;
> 4. the projection becomes defectless or Abhyankar;
> 5. the pro-isomorphism class changes.

The theorem must commute with smooth/etale base change and preserve immutable valuation ancestry.

## 4. Forbidden pseudo-ranks

The following do not provide intrinsic descent:

```text
number of approximants already consumed;
position in one key-polynomial sequence;
cofinal-tail shift;
fixed-projection defect factor;
finite constructible label eventually constant on the tail;
new token for every new model or exceptional divisor.
```
