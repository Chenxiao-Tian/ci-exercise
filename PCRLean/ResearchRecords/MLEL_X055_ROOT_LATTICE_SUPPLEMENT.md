## Unit Absorption and Terminal Root-Lattice Resolution

At a terminal radicial state the equations are

```text
z_j^(p^e_j) = u_j x^(b_j).
```

If `du_j` has a unit coefficient, the logarithmic Jacobian has maximal rank and the branch is regular. If `du_j=0`, smoothness over a perfect field gives `u_j=v_j^p`; the unit is absorbed into `z_j`. Repetition removes every invisible unit.

The remaining integral closure is the semigroup algebra obtained by adjoining the fractional characters `p^(-e_j)b_j` to the boundary lattice. Let

```text
M' = M + sum_j Z p^(-e_j)b_j.
```

The normalization is the toroidal embedding for the original cone in the saturated lattice `M'`. A finite regular subdivision is chosen simultaneously for all root relations and all marked colours. The labelled toroidal centre theorem realizes it by ordinary blowups in regular boundary strata of a smooth ambient pair.

This terminal theorem is independent of the chosen root equations because the lattice `M'` is the character lattice of the normalized torus of the finite radicial envelope.
