## The Transcendence-Defect Firewall

**Parent:** MLEL-X051 / FPR-R1C-HRR-FINAL  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** external generalized stability theorem and its exact programme consequence.

---

Let `(F/k,v)` be a valued function field over a perfect field with the trivial valuation. Write

```text
trdeg(F/k)
  >= rational-rank(vF/vk) + trdeg(Fv/kv).
```

The difference is the transcendence defect.

Kuhlmann's Generalized Stability Theorem states that a valued function field without transcendence defect over a defectless base is defectless. The trivially valued perfect ground field is defectless.

Therefore:

> **Firewall.** An Abhyankar or quasi-monomial valuation over the perfect ground field cannot carry the genuine independent class-V defect token.

Such a valuation belongs to the defectless/monomial branch and is handled by the finite toroidal preparation. The final valuation frontier is contained in positive transcendence defect.

This firewall is important for the global rank. One must not assign a class-V coordinate to every valuation; class V is reserved for the positive-transcendence-defect immediate algebraic layer that survives all coherent preparation.

The remaining reprojection theorem should lower transcendence defect, make the function field valuation-algebraic of transcendence degree one over a lower-dimensional base, or expose a coherent shadow. A fixed-projection defect factor is not a substitute for this drop.
