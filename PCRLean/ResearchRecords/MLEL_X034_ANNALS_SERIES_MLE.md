### Intrinsic Frobenius-Hasse States, Affine Conormal Packets, and Projectivity Discriminants

exceptional ledgers; proves the differential-integral equivalence and finite

Hasse-Morita and passive row cores; and turns finite Noetherian operator orbits
into presentation-independent affine conormal packets.  The affine equation
problem is encoded by a finite map `phi : N -> E`, target `b`, cokernel `Q`, and
obstruction `omega=[b]`.  The zero branch is recognized by maximal minors and
augmented minors.  The nonzero branch is controlled by the intrinsic order

## Paper III — 120 pages

### Joint Legality, All-Chart Transforms, and Hereditary Reentry without Reset

Every centre and every stratum used in Paper II must satisfy all gates
simultaneously: marked-power containment for all active owners; passive Tor
safety and normal flatness; SNC boundary compatibility; properness and
nonidentity.  This paper constructs the ordinary blowups, gives explicit
algebra on all standard charts and overlaps, controls strict and controlled
transforms, and records bounded exceptional debt.  Cleaning is a gauge arrow,
not geometric progress.  Hasse saturation, normalization, integral closure,
projectivity discriminants, order ideals, intersection posets and excess

reset.  The output is the All-Chart Hereditary Reentry theorem with terminal,

## Paper IV — 102 pages

### Fitting Defects, Recurrent Classes, and Causal Termination

This paper proves local termination.  Every new obstruction is assigned finite
source support and classified as envelope growth, fixed-carrier specialization,
pure exceptional debt, Frobenius/Newton degree change, owner renewal or
immediate defect.  Ancestral Pluecker-Fitting envelopes stabilize by
Noetherianity.  Exceptional debt has unique lineage and cannot be recharged.
Nonprojective conormal and cokernel packets are reduced by Schur-Fitting
residualization and kernel-regularization words.  Recurrent strongly connected
classes of finite transition types receive explicit exit macros.  Immediate
valuation defect is attached to a finite-type carrier and removed without a
projection.  The strict drops are combined into a well-founded product of
support dimension, Fitting multisets, kernel profiles, source ordinals, debt
ages, centre-word remainder and defect rank.
