# MLEL-X044 Supplement

## Projective Residual Coefficients and the One-Step Contact Formula

**Parent:** `MLEL-X044 / COP-PCH-UCS-FINAL`  
**Date:** 2026-08-05  
**Status:** local theorem proof; global preparation and termination remain open.

---

## 1. Algebraic setup

Let `(R,m)` be a regular local ring and let

```text
J=(y_1,...,y_s,z_1,...,z_t)
```

be generated by part of a regular system of parameters. Let `K subset J` be finite with `sqrt(K)=J`. Assume

```text
L=(K+J^2)/J^2
```

is generated by the classes of `y_1,...,y_s`. After replacing the `y_i` by sections of `K` with those initial classes, put `H=(y_1,...,y_s) subset K`, pass to `Rbar=R/H`, and write

```text
Jbar=(z_1,...,z_t),
Kbar=(K+H)/H subset Jbar^2.
```

The residual initial ideal is

```text
Abar=in_(Jbar)(Kbar) subset Sym(Jbar/Jbar^2).
```

Since `sqrt(Kbar)=Jbar`, an irrelevant-ideal power lies in `Abar`.

## 2. Coefficient ideals on projective normal directions

Let

```text
P=Proj Sym(Jbar/Jbar^2).
```

For each degree `d`, the piece `Abar_d` defines

```text
Abar_d tensor O_P(-d) -> O_P.
```

Let `c_d` be the image and `c_<=d=sum_(j<=d)c_j`. For `xi in P`, set

```text
nu(xi)=min{d | (c_<=d)_xi=O_(P,xi)}.
```

This is finite. The filtration is independent of the complement and normal frame because frame changes act through graded bundle automorphisms.

## 3. Blowup chart computation

On the `z_1`-chart, put `q=z_1` and divide the controlled intersection ideal by one exceptional factor. If

```text
f=f_d+f_(d+1)+...
```

is the `Jbar`-adic expansion, then

```text
q^(-1)f(q,qZ_2,...,qZ_t)
 =q^(d-1)(f_d(1,Z)+q f_(d+1)(1,Z)+...).
```

At a projective direction `xi`, every cumulative coefficient below `nu(xi)` is nonunit while one degree-`nu(xi)` coefficient is a unit. After the coefficient portfolio is universally exact and constant-profile, the successor residual order in the exceptional parameter is exactly `nu(xi)-1`.

## 4. Exactification requirement

The image ideals `c_d` and `c_<=d` need not commute with a nonflat preparation unless their source, image, and cokernel sequences are enrolled in the X042 exactified portfolio. Once those modules are flat, coefficient formation commutes with the actual carrier preparation.

## 5. Contact tree and bound

Let

```text
H=max_xi(nu(xi)-1).
```

Every surviving nonclean successor has contact height at most `H-1`. The maximum-height locus is a closed coefficient locus. Resolving its proper singular and Fitting boundary and repeating gives at most `H` genuine contact blowups along one source lineage; lower-dimensional preparatory words do not reset `H`.

## 6. Tangent-curve test

For

```text
K=(y,x^m) subset k[x,y],
```

one has `L=<y>`, residual direction `<x>`, `nu=m`, and height `m-1`. On the `x`-chart the controlled transform is

```text
(Y,x^(m-1)),
```

so the height becomes `m-2`.

## 7. Boundary

The strict formula assumes regular reduced support, locally free degree-one span, universally exact coefficient modules, constant coefficient profile, and a jointly legal support centre. These hypotheses are supplied conditionally by lower-dimensional full-portfolio preparation and X043 ambient realization. The theorem does not establish universal wild-branch coverage or global termination.
