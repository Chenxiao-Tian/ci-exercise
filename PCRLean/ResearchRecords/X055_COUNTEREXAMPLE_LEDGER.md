# X055 Counterexample and Defensive-Clause Ledger

1. **Tower dependence.** Sequential degree-p arguments are not automatically independent of generator order. X055 uses one simultaneous normalized Frobenius envelope and proves common-frame comparison.
2. **Invisible terminal units.** A unit cannot simply be discarded. It is absorbed only after `du=0` and the smooth p-constant lemma; otherwise its differential gives regularity.
3. **Singular toric centres.** A normalized toric modification is not itself an allowed final operation. The labelled toroidal theorem realizes a regular fan refinement by ordinary blowups in regular ambient boundary strata.
4. **Alteration as output.** Temkin's purely inseparable alteration is an intermediate input. The final output remains a sequence of ordinary blowups on the original smooth ambient scheme.
5. **Same-dimensional circularity.** The radicial theorem uses lower-dimensional centre preparation and same-dimensional inner ranks, not general resolution in the same dimension.
6. **Arbitrary projection choice.** Pointwise projections and compactness subcovers are noncanonical. The full defect-zero projection groupoid and complete Cech descent are retained for functoriality.
7. **Kangaroo and maximal-contact failure.** The rank does not use residual order or maximal contact alone; it retains Frobenius, projective contact, Fitting, source, and history data.
8. **Fixed jets.** The passive normal system is the complete homogenized associated graded, not a fixed finite jet.
9. **Normalized blowup loops.** Normalization and saturation are comparison operations, never counted as geometric descent or substituted for ordinary centre words.
