# X050 Counterexample and Failure-Boundary Ledger

## 1. Full wild monomialization is false

There are finite separable defect extensions of positive-characteristic function fields for which local and even weak local monomialization fail along a valuation. Hence a universal theorem requiring the complete finite extension to become monomial after birational changes is false.

## 2. Finite-state preparation is weaker

Preparing finitely many marked ideals, Hasse coefficients, modules and comparison maps does not monomialize the whole valued-field extension. This distinction is essential and permanent.

## 3. No best generator in the defect case

For a genuine Artin--Schreier defect extension, every finite list of defining right-hand sides admits a better one. Any algorithm which freezes one representative as terminal is unsound.

## 4. A finite list cannot represent the best-generator ideal

The ideal generated by the reciprocals of finitely many representatives is principal. The full best-generator ideal is not finitely generated in the defect case. Replacing the latter by a finite subideal loses the nonattained cut.

## 5. Coherent contraction is not the full valuation ideal

A contraction of the best-generator ideal to a Noetherian model is coherent, but its extension back to the valuation ring is principal and cannot equal the non-finitely-generated full ideal. It is only a finite shadow.

## 6. Noetherian ACC does not terminate invisible improvements

ACC terminates strict enlargements of one coherent contraction. Better generators may continue after that contraction stabilizes. These improvements are invisible to the frozen finite model and cannot be counted as coherent progress.

## 7. Cofinal tails are the same pro-object

A cofinal sequence of better generators and any tail define the same cut/pro-object. The number of improvements already made is not an intrinsic rank.

## 8. Fixed-projection defect is constant

Birational changes of models do not change the defect factor of a fixed valued-field extension. A rank cannot claim that this defect decreases from blowup to blowup.

## 9. Dependent and independent defect must not be merged

Dependent Artin--Schreier defect is connected to a purely inseparable immediate extension and belongs to the radicial branch. Independent defect is the residual nonattained-cut class. A single undifferentiated defect token hides the available geometric route.

## 10. New coherent shadows do not prove cut escape

A newly effective coefficient or ramification ideal can be prepared by X049, but this only handles the visible part. Uniform Nonattained-Cut Escape must also cover the stable situation in which no new coherent contraction appears.

## 11. Convex-subgroup height is conditional

Independent defect distances are often organized by idempotent cuts and isolated subgroups, but a strict decrease of subgroup height requires an actual uniform reprojection or centre theorem. Writing down the height without proving that geometric step is not termination.

## 12. Truth boundary

The best-generator ideal criterion and the full-monomialization no-go are rigorous. Finite-state preparation is conditional on the existing centre/transform architecture. Uniform Nonattained-Cut Escape and the final class-V ordinal remain open.
