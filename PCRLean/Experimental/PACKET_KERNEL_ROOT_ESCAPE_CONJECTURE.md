# PKRE-001: Packet-Kernel / Frobenius-Root / Escape Trichotomy

**Line:** PCR-JUMP-LEAN 2.0  
**Branch:** `pcr-jump-packet-boundary-20260803`  
**Class:** experimental conjecture architecture  
**Evidence:** E1 exact definitions plus local Lean target; not a theorem  
**Global status:** `OPEN_GAP`

## 1. Red-team observation

The current finite-packet algebra proves substantially more regularity than was
initially expected: for a finite-dimensional vector space `V` over a field and
any finite linear packet, the ideal generated by the common linear kernel has a
regular symmetric-algebra quotient.

This does **not** yet produce a legal blowup centre.  A legal affine centre must
satisfy both

```text
ideal != bottom   -- the closed centre is not the whole ambient space
ideal != top      -- the closed centre is not empty
```

as well as marked permissibility, singular-locus containment, boundary
transversality, passive safety, and hereditary transform laws.

The identity packet is the minimal falsifier:

```text
packet map = id
kernel     = 0
centre ideal = bottom
quotient is regular
```

Thus

```text
regular quotient  -/->  legal nontrivial centre.
```

## 2. Concrete examples driving the conjecture

### E1. `y^p - x^(p+1)`

At the origin in characteristic `p`, the singular locus is `x = 0`.  The pure
`y^p` term is invisible to ordinary first derivatives, while the `x^(p+1)`
term produces the first non-Frobenius defect.  The expected intrinsic packet
should retain the `y` tangent direction and generate the normal equation `x`.
This is the model for the strict-kernel branch.

### E2. A pure prime-power form `L^(p^e)`

The ordinary polar packet can vanish or become full-rank in an unhelpful
presentation, but the marked equation has the actual root hyperplane `(L)` and
belongs to `(L)^(p^e)`.  Blowing up a bottom kernel ideal is wrong; Frobenius
root extraction is the correct branch.

### E3. Zero packet

A zero packet has full common kernel and cuts out the origin in the linear
model.  This can be useful for an isolated maximal point, but it cannot by
itself choose a coherent centre on a nonisolated singular locus.  It exposes
the globalization and singular-locus-containment obligations.

### E4. Full separating packet

A packet separating every direction has bottom centre ideal.  Such a packet
must trigger either a terminal/smooth conclusion, a Frobenius-root reduction,
or a packet refinement.  It cannot trigger an ordinary blowup in the whole
ambient space.

### E5. Several owners

Each owner may have a nonzero common kernel while the joint packet is
injective.  Therefore owner aggregation can destroy the centre.  The correct
fallback may be a finite owner-safe centre word rather than the kernel of the
naive union packet.

## 3. Candidate trichotomy

Let `Sigma` be a prepared Hasse-saturated marked/Rees state on a smooth local
chart, and let

```text
E_Sigma : V_Sigma -> Q_Sigma
```

be the finite intrinsic packet-evaluation morphism on the relevant linearized
state.  Let

```text
I_Sigma = span(iota(ker E_Sigma)) subset Sym(V_Sigma).
```

The proposed trichotomy is:

### K. Strict-kernel branch

```text
bottom < I_Sigma < top.
```

Then `I_Sigma` is an actual regular linear centre algebraically.  The missing
bridge theorem must prove:

```text
marked permissibility
+ singular-locus containment
+ joint active/passive legality
+ boundary SNC
+ all-chart hereditary reentry.
```

### R. Frobenius-root branch

```text
I_Sigma = bottom
```

but the top marked layer is Frobenius/quasilinear after the permitted cleaning
and exceptional monomial removal.  Then construct an actual root ideal `J`
with

```text
topEquationIdeal <= J^q
```

and use `J`, not `I_Sigma`, as the centre or as the first word in a finite
centre macro.

### E. Escape/refinement branch

If the packet is injective and no Frobenius-root certificate exists, construct
one of:

1. a lower-order Hasse defect enlarging the common kernel;
2. a Fitting-rank drop and a new determinant chart;
3. an earlier strict Hilbert-Samuel/order/dimension drop;
4. an owner-conflict obstruction producing a finite centre word;
5. a radicial higher-Frobenius packet in the rank-zero chamber.

The branch must strictly decrease a declared packet/Fitting/history rank.

## 4. Strong bridge conjecture

### CONJ-PKRE-001

For every nonterminal prepared state over a perfect field of positive
characteristic, after finite intrinsic packetization and finite Hasse
saturation, exactly one of the following can be certified on a finite
principal-open atlas:

1. a strict intrinsic kernel ideal gives an actual permissible regular centre;
2. a prime-power Frobenius root gives an actual permissible regular centre;
3. a finite refinement/escape word produces an earlier strict geometric drop
   and reenters the same state category.

All chart choices are related by exact kernel-ideal transport, and the branch
classification cannot reset after cleaning, saturation, normalization, or
integral closure.

This conjecture is much stronger than the currently proved linear algebra and
may be false without additional preparation or owner hypotheses.

## 5. Minimal falsifiers

The conjecture must be rejected or weakened if any of the following occurs:

- an injective packet with no Frobenius root and no strict lower Hasse/Fitting
  defect;
- a strict kernel ideal not contained in the marked singular locus;
- two packet presentations with equal operator orbit but nonisomorphic
  hereditary transforms;
- a passive Tor witness destroyed by the kernel centre;
- a side chart in which the packet kernel becomes bottom without a rank drop;
- a quasilinear rank-zero family whose root ideals do not glue;
- an infinite sequence of packet refinements with no finite Noetherian witness.
