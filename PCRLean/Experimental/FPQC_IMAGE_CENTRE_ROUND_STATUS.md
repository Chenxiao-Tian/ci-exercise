# FPQC/Fitting image-centre round status

This experimental round adds a module-image construction of the global centre,
finite-packet canonicity, gauge-valued overlap descent, basic-open and faithfully
flat active/Frobenius descent, flat-passive Tor safety, graph-boundary
compatibility, immutable exceptional-debt lineage, overlap cocycles and causal
cleanup.

The round remains `OPEN_GAP`. In particular it does not prove universal
extraction of the module morphism and local graph atlas, nonflat passive safety,
scheme-level blowup base change, hereditary next-packet reconstruction,
immediate-defect escape, finite global serialization or the general resolution
theorem.

No declaration in this round is promoted into the official certified baseline
without a separate explicit promotion command and independent audit.
